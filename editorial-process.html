Methodology and editorial process

Every GA Forum ranking is built from the vendors' own pages, opened on the review date shown at the top, and the order is our verdict.

1. Pick the niche and the reader

Each ranking covers one job (for example anti-cheat for PC multiplayer games, or multistreaming for creators) and names the reader it is written for. A ranking that tries to serve everyone serves no one.

2. Build the long list

We list the products that are currently sold or maintained for that job, including open-source projects and large platforms. Products that are discontinued, parked or cannot be confirmed on a vendor page are dropped.

3. Check every product at the source

For each product we open the vendor's website, pricing page, license terms and documentation, and record what they state: who makes it, where the company is based (only when a legal or about page says so), pricing, free options, platforms and features. Those pages are listed under every entry as "Sources checked". We do not use review sites, scraped data or figures we only saw in search results.

4. Order by fit

We weigh the facts against the reader's situation: license and pricing terms at their scale, platform and engine support, setup effort, lock-in and data export, and how well the product covers the job. We do not run lab benchmarks and do not claim to; when a vendor makes a performance claim we label it as the vendor's.

5. Write the weaknesses

Every entry says where the product falls short and who should skip it. A ranking with only praise is an advertisement.

6. Data pages

Research pages only use figures from pages we opened, each with its publisher, year and a link. When a figure cannot be traced to an opened page, it is left out.

7. Updates and corrections

Rankings show a "last reviewed" date. When a vendor changes pricing or a product is discontinued, we update the page and the date. Report errors through the contact form; vendors can send corrections with a link to the page that proves them.

8. AI assistance

Research and drafting are AI-assisted. The rules above apply to every page, and the editor is responsible for what is published.

9. Money and independence

Vendors can pay for more visibility; how that works is explained in our disclosure, linked above every ranking. No commission is earned on clicks or purchases, and we do not work as an affiliate. See the disclosure.
