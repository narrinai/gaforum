How we work

Every product on a ranking was checked on its own website, documentation or pricing page on the date shown, and those pages are listed under the entry. The order is our verdict, each product gets a written weakness, and there are no scores out of ten. Vendors can pay for more visibility; how that works is explained in our disclosure, linked above every ranking. No commission is earned on clicks or purchases, and we do not work as an affiliate.
