Video Game Industry Market Size Statistics 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Players in 16 European markets spent about €29.7 billion on games in 2025, according to Video Games Europe, while US consumers spent $59.3 billion in 2024 according to the ESA. This page collects the most recent market size figures we could open on 18 November 2026, grouped by region and by company type.

Each line names the period and the group it covers, so figures from different publishers are not mixed up. For tools that track this kind of data, see market intelligence tools for games.

United States: spending and players

Figures from the Entertainment Software Association (ESA), a US trade group for game publishers.

  • Total US consumer spending on video games was $59.3 billion in 2024, according to the ESA's 2025 Essential Facts report. Source: ESA, 2025 Essential Facts About the U.S. Video Game Industry (2025, official)
  • Within the $59.3 billion US total for 2024, $51.3 billion went to content, $4.9 billion to hardware and $3.2 billion to accessories. Source: ESA, 2025 Essential Facts About the U.S. Video Game Industry (2025, official)
  • The ESA's 2025 report counted more than 205 million Americans who play video games. Source: ESA, 2025 Essential Facts About the U.S. Video Game Industry (2025, official)
  • The ESA reports that 212.3 million Americans play video games every week as part of their regular routine, in the archive page for its 2026 report. Source: ESA, Archive of the Annual Essential Facts report (2026, official)
  • Among US players in the ESA's 2026 report, 53% are male and 46% are female. Source: ESA, 2026 Essential Facts About the U.S. Video Game Industry (2026, official)
  • In the ESA's 2025 report, 28% of US players were age 50 or older. Source: ESA, 2025 Essential Facts About the U.S. Video Game Industry (2025, official)
  • In the ESA's 2026 report, 75% of US parents say they play video games themselves. Source: ESA, 2026 Essential Facts About the U.S. Video Game Industry (2026, official)
  • The ESA's 2026 Economic Impact Report says the US video game industry supports more than 250,000 jobs and generates $95.8 billion in total economic impact. Source: ESA, 2026 Economic Impact Report (2026, official)
  • The ESA states that the US video game industry contributed more than $65 billion to US GDP in 2025. Source: ESA, 2026 Economic Impact Report (2026, official)
  • California holds over 36,000 US game industry jobs, 44% of industry employment, and the top five states (California, Washington, Texas, New York and Florida) together hold 74%, per the ESA. Source: ESA, 2026 Economic Impact Report (2026, official)

Europe: revenue, players and studios

Figures from Video Games Europe and the European Games Developer Federation.

  • Video Games Europe puts 2025 revenue across 16 European markets at €29.7 billion, with the split 91% digital and 9% physical. Source: Video Games Europe, All About Video Games: European Key Facts 2025 (2026, official)
  • An estimated 199 million people aged 6 to 75 played games in the 16 European markets in 2025, equal to 51% of that age group's population. Source: Video Games Europe, All About Video Games: European Key Facts 2025 (2026, official)
  • By device in 2025, smartphone and tablet account for 49% of revenue across the 16 markets, console for 30% and PC for 12%. Source: Video Games Europe, All About Video Games: European Key Facts 2025 (2026, official)
  • Video Games Europe counts 123,000 people employed in the European games sector and 8,700 studios in its 2025 key facts. Source: Video Games Europe, All About Video Games: European Key Facts 2025 (2026, official)
  • In the 2025 key facts, 46% of European players are women, 77% are adults and the average player age is 34. Source: Video Games Europe, All About Video Games: European Key Facts 2025 (2026, official)
  • The 2024 edition of the European report put revenue at €26.8 billion in key markets, 4% above 2023. Source: Video Games Europe, annual data report launch (21 August 2025) (2025, official)
  • In the 2024 edition, digital revenue was 90% of European game revenue, up from 85% in 2023. Source: Video Games Europe, annual data report launch (21 August 2025) (2025, official)
  • The 2024 edition counted a European games workforce of 116,419, up 1.8% on 2023. Source: Video Games Europe, annual data report launch (21 August 2025) (2025, official)

Global mobile spending

Sensor Tower tracks store revenue on iOS and Google Play.

  • Sensor Tower reports that in-app purchase and paid app revenue across iOS and Google Play reached $167 billion in 2025, up 10.6% year over year. Source: Sensor Tower, 2026 State of Mobile (2026, vendor-claim)
  • Mobile game in-app purchase revenue approached $82 billion in 2025, up 1.3% year over year, which Sensor Tower calls a third straight year of growth. Source: Sensor Tower, 2026 State of Mobile (2026, vendor-claim)
  • Sensor Tower's State of Gaming report puts 2025 mobile game in-app purchases at $82 billion, up 1.4%, or $1.62 of revenue per download. Source: PocketGamer.biz, coverage of Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026 (2026, study)
  • For the first time, non-game apps passed games in mobile in-app purchase revenue in 2025, with non-game revenue up 21% year over year. Source: Sensor Tower, 2026 State of Mobile (2026, vendor-claim)
  • The United States remained the largest mobile market by revenue in 2025, with consumers spending nearly $60 billion on iOS and Google Play. Source: Sensor Tower, 2026 State of Mobile (2026, vendor-claim)
  • Sensor Tower counts 52 billion game downloads in 2025 across mobile, PC and console, with 42 billion of them on Google Play. Source: Sensor Tower, State of Gaming 2026 (2026, vendor-claim)

Publisher and platform results, 2026

Results for the quarters ended 30 September 2026, taken from company releases or coverage of them.

  • Take-Two's net bookings fell 18% to $1.60 billion in its fiscal second quarter 2027, against $1.96 billion a year earlier. Source: Take-Two Interactive, fiscal Q2 2027 results (Business Wire, 9 November 2026) (2026, vendor-claim)
  • Take-Two reiterated an outlook of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion in net bookings for fiscal year 2027. Source: Take-Two Interactive, fiscal Q2 2027 results (Business Wire, 9 November 2026) (2026, vendor-claim)
  • Recurrent consumer spending, which Take-Two defines as virtual currency, add-on content, in-game purchases and in-game advertising, was 81% of its net bookings in the September 2026 quarter. Source: Take-Two Interactive, fiscal Q2 2027 results (Business Wire, 9 November 2026) (2026, vendor-claim)
  • Roblox reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $1.572 billion, up 16%, and bookings of $1.697 billion, down 12%. Source: Gaming.net, Roblox Q3 2026 results coverage (2026, study)
  • Roblox averaged 138 million daily active users in the third quarter of 2026, down 9% from a year earlier and up 12% from the previous quarter. Source: GGRecon, Roblox Q3 2026 results coverage (2026, study)
  • Unity reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $622 million, compared with $471 million in the third quarter of 2025. Source: Unity, third quarter 2026 results (5 November 2026) (2026, vendor-claim)
  • AppLovin reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $2,185 million, up 55% from $1,405 million a year earlier. Source: AppLovin, third quarter 2026 results (Business Wire, 4 November 2026) (2026, vendor-claim)
  • AppLovin's nine-month 2026 revenue reached $5,951 million, up 56% from $3,823 million in the same period of 2025. Source: AppLovin, third quarter 2026 results (Business Wire, 4 November 2026) (2026, vendor-claim)

Methodology

On 18 November 2026 we opened the ESA's 2025 and 2026 report pages and economic impact page, the Video Games Europe key facts pages, Sensor Tower's State of Mobile and State of Gaming blog pages, and company results for Take-Two, AppLovin and Unity. Roblox figures come from press coverage of its shareholder letter because the filing itself blocked our fetch tool.

Market sizes from trade bodies, store trackers and publishers measure different things (consumer spending, store revenue, company bookings) and cover different countries, so they should not be added together. Newzoo's free report page blocked automated access and is not used.

Figures from search snippets were dropped.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the video game industry in 2026?

No single figure exists, because sources count different things. The ESA put US consumer spending at $59.3 billion in 2024, Video Games Europe put 2025 revenue in 16 European markets at €29.7 billion, and Sensor Tower put mobile game store spending at about $82 billion in 2025. Newzoo's global total was not accessible for this page.

Is mobile bigger than console in Europe?

In the Video Games Europe data for 2025, smartphone and tablet generated 49% of revenue across 16 markets and console 30%. PC accounted for 12%. The split differs slightly in the five largest markets, where mobile was 48% and console 31%.

Why do US and European figures not match global estimates?

Each publisher defines scope differently: ESA counts US consumer spending on content, hardware and accessories, Video Games Europe counts revenue in named countries, and Sensor Tower counts only iOS and Google Play store revenue. Adding them would double count and miss markets.

Where does this data come from?

Every figure links to the page that contains it. Trade-body reports are official sources, company results are vendor claims about their own business, and press coverage is labeled as such in the source name.
