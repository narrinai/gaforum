Creator Economy and Streamer Statistics 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Two thirds of creators, 67%, earn less than $10,000 a year from content, according to the CreatorIQ and Influencers.Club State of Creators 2026 survey of 5,095 creators. At the platform level, Streams Charts tallied 31.698 billion hours of livestream viewing in Q3 2026, with YouTube at 43% and Twitch at 14.2%.

The figures below cover earnings, audience share, platform payout rules and fan-funding totals, each tied to a page we opened.

What creators earn

  • A State of Creators 2026 report from CreatorIQ and Influencers.club found that 67% of creators earn less than $10,000 a year from content creation. Source: TheWrap, CreatorIQ and Influencers.club State of Creators 2026 (2026, survey)
  • The same report, covering 5,095 creator respondents across 100 regions, found that 62% say content creation is not their primary source of income. Source: TheWrap, CreatorIQ and Influencers.club State of Creators 2026 (2026, survey)
  • CreatorIQ and influencers.club found that only 5% of surveyed creators clear $100,000 per year. Source: Tubefilter, CreatorIQ and influencers.club creator survey coverage (2026, survey)
  • 73% of surveyed creators handle everything themselves, and only 8% have a talent manager or agent. Source: Tubefilter, CreatorIQ and influencers.club creator survey coverage (2026, survey)
  • The Influencer Marketing Factory's 2026 report says 45.6% of surveyed creators earn between $10,000 and $100,000 a year. Source: Tubefilter, The Influencer Marketing Factory 2026 Creator Economy Report coverage (2026, survey)
  • 62% of creators in the Influencer Marketing Factory's survey of 1,000 respondents have been in their current careers for three years or less. Source: Tubefilter, The Influencer Marketing Factory 2026 Creator Economy Report coverage (2026, survey)

Sponsorships and trust

  • 46% of the 5,095 creators surveyed by CreatorIQ and influencers.club rely on brand deals for the bulk of their income. Source: Tubefilter, CreatorIQ and influencers.club creator survey coverage (2026, survey)
  • 35% of those creators name a lack of consistent brand deals as the number one barrier to growing, and more than 50% say their brand-deal earnings rose only slightly or stayed flat from 2025 to 2026. Source: Tubefilter, CreatorIQ and influencers.club creator survey coverage (2026, survey)
  • Only 15% of surveyed creators fully trust sponsored content from other creators when making a purchase, while 48% somewhat trust it depending on the creator. Source: Tubefilter, CreatorIQ and influencers.club creator survey coverage (2026, survey)
  • Among creators earning more than $250,000 a year, 60% named Instagram as their primary platform for branded content, compared with 30% for TikTok. Source: TheWrap, CreatorIQ and Influencers.club State of Creators 2026 (2026, survey)
  • 72% of surveyed creators use AI tools for brainstorming, writing or editing, while 4% use them for strategy and 1% to automate workflows. Source: TheWrap, CreatorIQ and Influencers.club State of Creators 2026 (2026, survey)

Livestream viewing by platform

Streams Charts tracks hours watched across public livestreaming platforms; the figures here are quarter over quarter against Q2 2026.

  • Streams Charts counted 31.698 billion hours watched across livestreaming platforms in Q3 2026, a record that grew only 0.8% on Q2. Source: Streamer.guide, Q3 2026 livestreaming report (Streams Charts data) (2026, study)
  • YouTube led Q3 2026 with 13.62 billion hours, down 13.7% on the quarter, and its share fell from 49.5% to 43%. Source: Streamer.guide, Q3 2026 livestreaming report (Streams Charts data) (2026, study)
  • TikTok LIVE reached 10.6 billion hours in Q3 2026, up 19%, for a 33.5% share of tracked watch time. Source: Streamer.guide, Q3 2026 livestreaming report (Streams Charts data) (2026, study)
  • Twitch recorded 4.51 billion hours in Q3 2026, up 4%, for a 14.2% share, compared with 13.8% in Q2. Source: Streamer.guide, Q3 2026 livestreaming report (Streams Charts data) (2026, study)
  • Kick grew 30.7% to 2.04 billion hours in Q3 2026 at a 6.4% share, which left Twitch 2.2 times Kick's size against 2.8 times in Q2. Source: Streamer.guide, Q3 2026 livestreaming report (Streams Charts data) (2026, study)
  • Gaming made up 76.9% of Twitch hours and 16.2% of TikTok LIVE hours in Q3 2026. Source: Streamer.guide, Q3 2026 livestreaming report (Streams Charts data) (2026, study)

Twitch audience and monetization rules

  • Twitch reported more than 8.6 billion hours of gaming content watched between January 1 and September 1, 2026, using its own internal data. Source: Streamer.guide, Twitch State of Gaming 2026 (Twitch data) (2026, official)
  • Streams with the DropsEnabled tag generated 1.6 billion hours watched in Twitch's January to September 2026 window, up 46%, and the number of users claiming Drops grew 15% to 40 million. Source: Streamer.guide, Twitch State of Gaming 2026 (Twitch data) (2026, official)
  • Since May 13, 2026, Twitch makes Bits, subscriptions, emotes, badges and Channel Points available to eligible streamers without Affiliate or Partner status, though cash payouts still require Affiliate or Partner status and a $50 minimum. Source: Streamer.guide, Twitch monetization changes (May 2026) (2026, official)
  • Twitch's updated Affiliate criteria from June 2026 are 4 hours streamed, 4 different streaming days, 3 concurrent viewers on those days and 25 followers, down from 8 hours, 7 days and 50 followers. Source: Streamer.guide, Twitch monetization changes (May 2026) (2026, official)

YouTube and fan-funding payouts

  • YouTube said on August 11, 2026 that its Partner Program has more than 3 million creators and is receiving its first significant changes since 2018. Source: Google, New Opportunities to Earn and Changes to the YouTube Partner Program (2026, official)
  • From February 1, 2027, creators need 10 million qualified Shorts views over the last 90 days to be eligible for ads and subscription revenue sharing on Shorts. Source: Google, New Opportunities to Earn and Changes to the YouTube Partner Program (2026, official)
  • From February 1, 2027, new applicants to the YouTube Partner Program need 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. Source: Google, New Opportunities to Earn and Changes to the YouTube Partner Program (2026, official)
  • YouTube's Premium subscription pool for creators is 30% of net subscription revenue for Premium and 60% for Premium Lite, distributed by member watch time and views. Source: Google, New Opportunities to Earn and Changes to the YouTube Partner Program (2026, official)
  • Patreon announced in August 2025 that it had paid $10 billion to creators since launch, and its CEO said creators take in $2 billion per year through the platform. Source: Tubefilter, Patreon and Kajabi payout milestones (2025, vendor-claim)
  • Patreon counted 25 million paid memberships in August 2025, and Kajabi announced the same $10 billion cumulative payout milestone days apart. Source: Tubefilter, Patreon and Kajabi payout milestones (2025, vendor-claim)

Methodology

On 18 November 2026 we opened the TheWrap and Tubefilter coverage of the CreatorIQ and Influencers.Club survey, Tubefilter's summary of the Influencer Marketing Factory report, Google's YouTube Partner Program announcement, and Streamer.guide articles that republish Streams Charts and Twitch data. Streams Charts itself returned a block page, so those figures are second-hand.

Figures were kept only where the page states number, period and population.

Survey samples are self-selected creator panels run by vendors, so earnings distributions are indicative, not census data. The Twitch figures are Twitch internal data and are not comparable with third-party hours-watched trackers.

Patreon payout numbers are from August 2025 and were not refreshed. Social Blade and Hatchet were opened but publish no citable aggregate figures.

Frequently asked questions

What share of creators earn a living from content?

In the 2026 CreatorIQ and Influencers.Club survey of 5,095 creators, 62% said content is not their primary income and 67% earn under $10,000 a year. Only 5% earn $100,000 or more, according to the Tubefilter report.

Which platform has the most livestream viewing?

YouTube, at 13.62 billion hours and 43% of tracked watch time in Q3 2026 per Streams Charts, though it fell 13.7% on the quarter. TikTok LIVE had 33.5% and Twitch 14.2%.

Do I need Twitch Affiliate to turn on subs and Bits?

Not since May 13, 2026, according to Streamer.guide's summary of Twitch's announcement. Payouts still require Affiliate or Partner status and the $50 minimum. Check Twitch's help center for current terms in your region.

Are the Twitch hours comparable to Streams Charts hours?

No. Twitch's 8.6 billion hours cover gaming content from January 1 to September 1, 2026, from its own data. Streams Charts reports 4.51 billion hours for all Twitch content in Q3 alone, using its own tracking method.
