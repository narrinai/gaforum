Esports Audience and Revenue Statistics 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

The 2026 Esports World Cup in Paris reported 850 million viewers and 440 million hours watched, according to the organizer's own recap. Revenue is harder to pin down: two research firms opened for this page put the 2026 esports market at USD 2.6 billion and USD 2.98 billion, and both rank sponsorship as the largest revenue stream.

The facts below come from pages opened on 18 November 2026, and each one says who measured it, for which event or year, and how far the number can be compared.

Esports World Cup 2026 audience

These figures are organizer-reported and describe the seven-week event in Paris.

  • The Esports World Cup Foundation reported 850 million viewers and 440 million hours watched for EWC 2026, across more than 100 broadcast partners in 47 languages. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)
  • EWC 2026 sold 175,000 tickets and recorded 2 million visits across competition venues, Fan Fest and activations in Paris, according to the organizer. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)
  • About 5,000 co-streamers broadcast EWC 2026, and more than 350 creators attended on site, according to the organizer. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)
  • EWC 2026 production used 390 cameras and created more than 9,000 hours of live content, according to the organizer. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)

Prize money and competition scale

  • EWC 2026 offered a record USD 75 million prize pool across 25 tournaments, with more than 2,000 players from over 100 nationalities and 200+ clubs, according to the organizer. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)
  • The Road to EWC qualifier phase ahead of the 2026 event featured 1.5 million players competing across 330 tournaments. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)
  • More than 1,500 players took part in the Last Chance Qualifiers held in Paris for EWC 2026, covering titles such as Counter-Strike 2, Chess, Street Fighter 6 and TEKKEN 8. Source: Esports World Cup, 2026 Esports World Cup in Numbers (2026, official)
  • AG.AL won the EWC 2026 Club Championship with 5,300 points after competing in 18 competitions, including six podium finishes. Source: Esports World Cup, Trophy Award Ceremony report (2026, official)
  • MarkNtel Advisors reports that the 2025 Esports World Cup featured more than 2,000 professional players, 200 clubs, 25 tournaments and 24 games, with a prize pool above USD 70 million. Source: MarkNtel Advisors, Esports Market Research Report 2026 to 2032 (2026, study)

Pathways for tournament organizers

  • Applications for the Esports World Cup 2027 Direct Qualifier and Satellite Last Chance Qualifier programs are open until 20 December 2026. Source: Esports World Cup, 2027 Qualifier Program (2026, official)
  • All EWC 2027 Satellite Last Chance Qualifier events must conclude by 1 June 2027, ahead of the EWC 2027 Last Chance Qualifiers. Source: Esports World Cup, 2027 Qualifier Program (2026, official)
  • The Esports World Cup's FAQ lists Counter-Strike 2, Chess, Dota 2, VALORANT and FATAL FURY: City of the Wolves as confirmed games for EWC 2027, with more to be announced. Source: Esports World Cup, FAQ (2026, official)
  • The Esports World Cup Foundation's Club Partner Program, launched in 2023, gives partnered clubs no competitive advantage and requires them to use each game's qualification system. Source: Esports World Cup, FAQ (2026, official)

League of Legends Worlds 2026 viewership

Esports Charts figures exclude Chinese streaming platforms, so they are not comparable to totals that include them. Esports Charts itself blocks automated access, so these figures come from a news report that cites its data.

  • The Worlds 2026 Play-In peaked at 1,021,367 viewers according to Esports Charts data cited by Sheep Esports, a 59.22% decrease from 2025 and a 28.75% drop from 2024. Source: Sheep Esports, Worlds 2026 Play-In viewership (citing Esports Charts) (2026, study)
  • The 2025 Worlds Play-In peaked at 2,503,910 viewers, a record at the time, driven by a T1 and Invictus Gaming matchup, according to Sheep Esports citing Esports Charts. Source: Sheep Esports, Worlds 2026 Play-In viewership (citing Esports Charts) (2026, study)
  • Sheep Esports reported that Karmine Corp's run at Worlds 2026 helped double French viewership compared with the previous year, citing Esports Charts. Source: Sheep Esports, Worlds 2026 Play-In viewership (citing Esports Charts) (2026, study)

Market size and revenue mix

The two research firms below define the market differently and publish different totals, so treat them as a range, not one number.

  • Future Market Insights estimates the global esports market at USD 2.6 billion in 2026, up from USD 2.3 billion in 2025, with a forecast of USD 9.0 billion by 2036 at a 13.4% CAGR. Source: Future Market Insights, eSports Market 2026 to 2036 (2026, study)
  • Future Market Insights attributes 48.0% of 2026 esports revenue to sponsorships and brand endorsements. Source: Future Market Insights, eSports Market 2026 to 2036 (2026, study)
  • Future Market Insights estimates multiplayer online battle arena games at 44.0% of the 2026 esports market and PC gaming at 58.0% by platform. Source: Future Market Insights, eSports Market 2026 to 2036 (2026, study)
  • MarkNtel Advisors values the esports market at USD 2.98 billion in 2026, up from USD 2.23 billion in 2025, with USD 7.28 billion forecast for 2032 at a 16.05% CAGR. Source: MarkNtel Advisors, Esports Market Research Report 2026 to 2032 (2026, study)
  • MarkNtel Advisors estimates sponsorship at about 38.7% of 2026 esports market revenue, the largest revenue-source segment. Source: MarkNtel Advisors, Esports Market Research Report 2026 to 2032 (2026, study)
  • MarkNtel Advisors puts North America at a 50% share of the 2026 esports market. Source: MarkNtel Advisors, Esports Market Research Report 2026 to 2032 (2026, study)
  • MarkNtel Advisors reports that China's esports industry generated about USD 1.89 billion in the first half of 2026, up 2.78% year over year, with live streaming at 80.43% of that revenue. Source: MarkNtel Advisors, Esports Market Research Report 2026 to 2032 (2026, study)
  • MarkNtel Advisors reports that China's esports user base exceeded 495 million in 2025. Source: MarkNtel Advisors, Esports Market Research Report 2026 to 2032 (2026, study)

Methodology

On 18 November 2026 the Esports World Cup site (about page, FAQ, news and three articles), two market research report pages and one news article were opened. Esports Charts and Newzoo returned access errors to the fetch tool, so no figure is attributed directly to them; the Worlds figures are marked as reported by Sheep Esports from Esports Charts data.

Audience numbers for EWC 2026 are organizer-reported and not independently audited. Market-size figures are research-firm estimates behind summary pages: their definitions differ, and the full reports were not opened.

Prize pool figures differ between years and sources.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the esports market in 2026?

It depends on the definition. Future Market Insights estimates USD 2.6 billion and MarkNtel Advisors USD 2.98 billion. Both put sponsorship as the largest revenue stream, at 48.0% and 38.7% respectively.

Neither figure is audited company revenue.

How many people watched the Esports World Cup 2026?

The organizer reported 850 million viewers and 440 million hours watched across more than 100 broadcast partners. The measure of a viewer is the organizer's own, so compare it carefully with peak-concurrent figures from third parties.

Why do esports viewership numbers differ between sources?

Some trackers exclude Chinese platforms, some count only peak concurrent viewers, and some count total reach. Always check whether a figure is peak viewers, hours watched or unique viewers before comparing events.

How can a local organizer connect to the Esports World Cup?

The 2027 Qualifier Program accepts applications for Direct Qualifiers and Satellite Last Chance Qualifiers until 20 December 2026. Satellite events must finish by 1 June 2027.
