History of ga-forum.com

ga-forum.com was the original home of the Gaming-Age Forums, the vBulletin board that grew into NeoGAF. Today GA Forum continues on the domain as an independent software research site with its own forum.

GA Forum is not the former forum and has no connection to Gaming-Age, NeoGAF or their operators. No posts, threads or member data from the old board were copied. Everything below comes from public captures in the Internet Archive, linked per fact.

2004: Gaming-Age Forums

The oldest archived capture of ga-forum.com, from June 10, 2004, shows a vBulletin board titled "Gaming-Age Forums" under the menu of the Gaming-Age news site (news, previews, reviews, specials, cheats). It listed two forums: Gaming Discussion and Off-Topic Discussion. That day the board counted 616 threads, 13,314 posts and 1,573 members, with a record of 382 users online at once. (Archive capture, June 10, 2004)

2005 to 2006: rapid growth as "GAF"

By January 1, 2006 the page title read "GAF - powered by vBulletin" and the counters showed 77,869 threads, 2,494,938 posts and 9,152 members; the record for users online was 2,514, set on May 27, 2005. (Archive capture, January 1, 2006)

Between May and September 2005 a copy of the board also ran on new.ga-forum.com; from October 2005 that host redirected to www.ga-forum.com. (Archive capture of the redirect)

2006 to 2008: the move to neogaf.com

The earliest archived redirect from ga-forum.com to neogaf.com/forum is dated April 5, 2006, and later captures through 2010 show the same redirect. (Archive capture, April 5, 2006)

Between June and October 2008 the old domain again showed the board itself, now titled "NeoGAF - Powered by vBulletin", with forums for FAQ, Gaming Discussion and Off-Topic Discussion. On June 1, 2008 it counted 301,579 threads, 11,234,191 posts and 35,216 members, with a record of 13,071 users online on March 1, 2008. (June 1, 2008, October 23, 2008)

From December 17, 2008 the domain root answered with a permanent redirect to neogaf.com/forum. (Archive capture, December 17, 2008)

The page titles in these captures trace the name: "Gaming-Age Forums" in 2004, "GAF" in 2005 and 2006, "NeoGAF" in 2008.

2011 to 2026: expiry, a stray blog and parking

A capture from June 6, 2011 shows a registrar notice that the domain had expired on June 5, 2011. (Archive capture)

From 2013 to 2017 the domain carried an unrelated WordPress blog, first the default "Just another WordPress site" and later general lifestyle posts with no link to games. (2013, 2016, 2017) After that it was parked until 2026. (2018 capture)

2026: GA Forum

In November 2026 the domain came back as GA Forum: software rankings, comparisons, guides and data for game developers, streamers and esports organizers, plus a forum where readers can discuss them. Old forum links (threads, member pages, archive pages) no longer exist; thread and forum links now point here, and links to member and posting pages return "not found".

Looking for the community that grew up here? It continued on neogaf.com, which is a separate site we are not affiliated with.
