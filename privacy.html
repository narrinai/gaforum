Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what personal data GA Forum (G-Cas B.V., Fladderiepvliet 3, 3545 AJ Utrecht, Netherlands, KvK 42008661) processes, why, and your rights under the GDPR. Contact: hello@ga-forum.com.

Reading the site

The site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Our host (Netlify) processes standard server logs (IP address, browser, requested page) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Contact and get-listed forms

When you send a form we receive your name, email address, company and message. We use them to answer you and, for get-listed requests, to discuss a listing. Form messages are stored by Netlify and in our mailbox, and an automatic confirmation is sent through SendGrid (Twilio). Legal basis: your request (pre-contractual steps) and our legitimate interest in answering. We keep this correspondence for up to two years after the last contact.

Forum accounts

To post in the forum you sign in with your email address. We send a one-time six-digit code to that address through SendGrid. We store:

  • your email address (to sign you in and to contact you about your account or moderation; never shown publicly);
  • the display name you choose (public, shown with your posts);
  • your posts, edits and the time they were made (public unless held or removed by moderation);
  • reports you file on posts (visible only to moderators);
  • a hashed copy of each login code (deleted after it expires or is used), a session cookie, and short-lived rate-limit counters keyed to your email address and IP address to stop abuse.

Forum data is stored in Netlify Blobs on Netlify's infrastructure. Legal basis: providing the forum you signed up for (contract) and our legitimate interest in preventing spam and abuse.

Cookie: signing in sets one strictly necessary cookie, gaf_session (HttpOnly, Secure, 30 days), that keeps you logged in. It is not used for tracking.

Retention: your account and posts stay until you ask us to delete them. On request we delete your account and email address; your posts are then removed or shown as written by a deleted user, at your choice.

Processors

Netlify, Inc. (hosting, forms, functions, storage) and Twilio SendGrid (email delivery). Both may process data outside the EU under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, by emailing hello@ga-forum.com. You may also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
