# Category ranking brief (GA Forum)

Every ranking page on ga-forum.com is generated by `scripts/build.py` from one file: `data/categories/<slug>.json`.
`python3 scripts/validate_categories.py <slug>` must print `OK`.

## What the site is
GA Forum is an independent software research site and discussion forum for people who make games, run livestreams and organize esports: indie and mid-size studio leads, technical directors, producers, community managers, streamers and their editors, event and broadcast teams, tournament organizers. Global, American English.

## Inputs you get
- The plan entry for the page (slug, title, angle, candidate tools with domains): `plan.json` in the scratchpad (path in your task).
- Verified vendor facts: `data/facts/index.json` (a map tool slug -> fact record, built from pages opened today). Each record has `status`, `site`, `company`, `hq`, `country`, `what_it_is`, `platforms`, `pricing`, `pricing_model`, `free_plan`, `key_features`, `notable`, `facts` [{fact, url}].

## Hard rules
1. **Facts only from the facts file** (or from vendor pages you open yourself with the fetch helper named in your task, which you then add to `sources`). Never write a price, limit, platform, customer, founding year, country or feature that is not in the tool's fact record or a page you opened. If pricing is unknown, say so plainly ("pricing is not published; the vendor sells on quote").
2. Only tools with `status: "verified"` may be ranked. **A page needs at least 10 verified tools.** If the planned list has fewer than 10 verified tools, you may add other real tools in the same niche ONLY if you open their vendor page yourself with the fetch helper and record facts; otherwise do not write the file and report the page as held, with the count.
3. Every tool gets `sources`: 2 to 4 objects `{"fact": "...", "url": "..."}` copied from its fact record (or pages you opened). URLs must be vendor pages that were actually opened.
4. Do not invent: no scores out of ten, no user counts, no test results we did not run ("we tested" is forbidden; we read vendor documentation and pricing pages, compared published terms, and weighed fit). Say "according to the vendor" for vendor claims.
5. No em dashes (U+2014) or en dashes used as dashes; use commas, colons, parentheses or full stops. Never write "top 10" (if needed say "the top three" or "top seven"). No exclamation marks. No hype words: robust, seamless, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changer, game changer, revolutionize, unleash, elevate, supercharge, best-in-class, "in today's", "look no further", "dive in", "level up your".
6. American English. Dollar prices as stated by the vendor (keep the vendor's currency if not USD).
7. Originality: do not open or paraphrase other review sites or our sister sites (theknowledgeengineers.com, theinterregnum.net, aitoolcompare.ai, europeanpurpose.com, sameteampartners.com). Write from the facts.
8. **Overlap gate: pages of the same type may share at most 15% of their 6-word sequences.** So every page needs its own sentence frames. Do not reuse a stock frame across tools or pages ("X is a strong choice for", "stands out for its", "is best suited for teams that", "If you need X, Y is", "comes with a steep learning curve", "pricing is not published"). Vary sentence openings per tool. Write the criteria headings, FAQ questions and steps for THIS niche only.
9. Order is the verdict. #1 to #3 go to the best fit for the page's angle and reader (an indie or mid-size studio, a working streamer, a tournament organizer, or a corporate event team, depending on the niche), based on facts. Big platforms (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Adobe, Zendesk-size incumbents) may rank anywhere if they truly fit.

## File shape (strict JSON, UTF-8, 2-space indent, ensure_ascii false)
```json
{
  "slug": "best-2d-game-engines",
  "name": "2D game engines",
  "title": "Best 2D Game Engines in 2026",
  "group": "Game development",
  "hub": "games",
  "published": "2026-11-18",
  "reviewed": "2026-11-18",
  "summary_line": "One sentence, 15-25 words: what the category is for (used in llms.txt and the meta description).",
  "definition": "45-80 words. A crisp 'What are 2D game engines?' definition an AI assistant could quote.",
  "intro": "110-170 words: the reader's real problem and how this ranking judged the tools (what was read: vendor docs, pricing, license terms).",
  "criteria": [ {"heading": "Niche-specific heading", "text": "60-110 words"} ],
  "market_notes": ["2 paragraphs, 60-110 words each: what the list itself shows (counts you can verify from the facts: how many publish prices, how many are open source, how many have a free plan, where they are based), plus trade-offs in this niche."],
  "how_to_choose": [ {"title": "Short imperative step", "text": "60-100 words"} ],
  "faqs": [ {"q": "Question a buyer would ask an AI assistant", "a": "40-90 words, answer first"} ],
  "related": ["4-8 other category slugs from the plan that a reader would need next"],
  "tools": [
    {
      "slug": "gamemaker",
      "name": "GameMaker",
      "site": "https://gamemaker.io/",
      "company": "YoYo Games Ltd",
      "country": "United Kingdom",
      "tagline": "Under 12 words: what it is",
      "best_for": "3-8 words",
      "pricing": "One sentence from the fact record",
      "pricing_model": "Short label from the fact record",
      "free_plan": "Free plan | Free trial | Open source | Free | None | Unknown",
      "platforms": "short, from the facts, or empty string",
      "features": ["4-6 short feature names from the facts"],
      "summary": "18-30 words: why it sits at this position.",
      "verdict": "60-100 words: what it does well, for whom, why this position.",
      "weaknesses": "30-60 words: what it does badly or lacks (facts-based: license limits, missing platforms, no published price, Windows only, etc.).",
      "wrong_for": "20-40 words: who should skip it and what to look at instead (name another tool on this page).",
      "sources": [ {"fact": "...", "url": "https://..."} ]
    }
  ]
}
```
Counts: 4 criteria, 2 market_notes, 3 how_to_choose steps, 7 FAQs (the first one is "What is the best <name> in 2026?" naming #1 and when #2 or #3 is better), 10 to 14 tools. `hub` is one of `games`, `livestreaming`, `esports` (given in your task). Total prose about 2,600-3,600 words.

## Adding a tool you researched yourself
If you open vendor pages yourself (to replace a dropped tool or to reach 10), open them with the fetch helper (`python3 /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-sebastiaansmits-Documents-europeanpurpose/50ccc031-f8ba-4c2c-b848-796b01078711/scratchpad/gaforum/gather/fetch.py URL 6000`; WebFetch is rate-limited) and write the fact records (same format as data/facts/index.json records, status "verified", 3+ facts with the URLs you opened) to `data/facts/extra_<category-slug>.json` (a JSON list). Then run `python3 scripts/merge_facts.py` before validating. A tool record with status "dropped" in index.json may be re-checked this way if you can open its vendor page (some sites block one fetcher but not another).

## Before you finish
Run `python3 scripts/merge_facts.py` (if you added facts), `python3 scripts/validate_categories.py <slug>` for each of your files until it prints OK, and `python3 scripts/check_overlap.py` (prints the worst overlap per category page; yours must be under 15% against every other page; `--show a b` prints shared phrases). Fix by rewriting sentences, not by deleting content.
