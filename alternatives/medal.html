Medal Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Medal is a game clip app for PC that records, edits and shares gameplay clips, and its homepage describes it as free to use. People who leave it usually want one of three things: capture that lives inside software they already run, a recorder without an account or sharing layer, or a tool that cuts clips from a live stream instead of from local gameplay.

This page lists nine alternatives, from a GPU vendor app to an open source recorder to AI clippers for Twitch, YouTube and Kick, and says where each one differs from Medal.

Ordering: editorial, based on what each vendor publishes about pricing, licensing and features as of the date above. What pushes teams away from Medal

  • You want capture handled by the graphics driver app you already install, such as the NVIDIA App or AMD Software Adrenalin, instead of a separate clipping client.
  • You prefer software with a free and open source license, so nothing about the recorder depends on a company account.
  • Your clips come from live broadcasts, and cutting vertical shorts from a stream VOD fits better than recording local gameplay.
  • You need a Windows recorder with a paid license and a free trial, for example for longer sessions or a frame rate target.
  • You only need quick screenshots and short screen clips, and a game-focused app feels like more than the job requires.

What to use instead

  1. 1

    Outplayed

    Auto-captured plays with an editor

    Free option
    Free

    Outplayed is the closest match to Medal's idea: it captures your best plays on its own while you play, then gives you an editor and performance tracking in the same app. It also has a free tier and a Premium tier that removes ads.

    Switch to it if you like the clip-first workflow but want a different interface or editor. Expect the same trade as with Medal, which is that you run a dedicated capture client next to your game.

    Visit Outplayed

  2. 2

    NVIDIA App

    GeForce users who want built-in capture

    Free option
    Unknown

    The NVIDIA App is the driver companion for PC gamers and creators, and NVIDIA lists ShadowPlay and the Broadcast App among its features. If you already own a GeForce card you install it anyway, which makes capture one less program to maintain.

    You give up the social sharing side that clip apps build around. It only makes sense on NVIDIA hardware, so check your GPU before counting on it.

    Checked on nvidia.com

    Visit NVIDIA App

  3. 3

    OBS Studio

    Full control over recording scenes

    Free option
    Open source

    OBS Studio is free and open source recording and live streaming software for Windows 10 and 11, macOS 13.0 or newer, and Linux. Scenes can combine window captures, browser windows, webcams and capture cards.

    That is far more control than a clip app offers, and it costs nothing. The price is setup time: you build scenes and settings yourself, and nothing here finds highlights for you.

    Checked on obsproject.com

    Visit OBS Studio

  4. 4

    Bandicam

    Windows recording with a paid full version

    Free option
    Free

    Bandicam records the PC screen, gameplay and webcam, with built-in editing and AI tools, and it is sold as a free version and a full version. The site links a page comparing the two.

    Choose it if you want a conventional recorder with a paid upgrade path rather than an ad-supported clip client. The opened pages did not list prices, so confirm the full-version cost on the vendor site before buying.

    Checked on bandicam.com

    Visit Bandicam

  5. 5

    Action!

    High frame rate game recording on Windows

    Free option
    Free trial

    Action! from Mirillis records and streams desktop, apps and games with webcam and microphone. Its homepage claims recording at 120 FPS with low CPU usage and offers a free trial.

    It runs on Windows Vista through Windows 11. It suits people who care about frame rate in recordings and are happy to pay for a license.

    It has no community sharing layer, so sending a clip means exporting the file yourself.

    Checked on mirillis.com

    Visit Action!

  6. 6

    AMD Software Adrenalin

    Radeon users who want in-driver capture

    Free option
    Unknown

    AMD Software Adrenalin Edition is the application for AMD products that gives access to software features, game stats and driver updates. AMD says it also lets you capture in-game moments and launch installed games from one place.

    For Radeon owners it is the counterpart to the NVIDIA App. As with that option, you trade Medal's sharing community for a tool tied to your graphics hardware.

    Checked on amd.com

    Visit AMD Software Adrenalin

  7. 7

    Eklipse

    Turning streams into vertical clips

    Free option
    Free plan

    Eklipse is an AI clip maker that turns Twitch, YouTube and Kick streams into vertical clips with captions, and schedules them to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. It starts from a stream, not from your local game footage, so it replaces Medal only if your audience lives on short video.

    Its pricing page lists free features such as an auto clipper and a YouTube clipper, which makes a trial cheap.

    Checked on eklipse.gg

    Visit Eklipse

  8. 8

    StreamLadder

    Cutting stream highlights for short video

    Free option
    Free plan

    StreamLadder uses AI to find strong moments in a Twitch, YouTube or Kick stream and edits them for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Its pricing page lists Starter at $15, Creator at $27 and Pro at $39 a month, plus a free plan.

    Pick it if your goal is a steady flow of short clips from broadcasts. It will not capture your gameplay locally the way Medal does.

    Checked on streamladder.com

    Visit StreamLadder

  9. 9

    ShareX

    Free screenshots and short screen recordings

    Free option
    Open source

    ShareX is a free and open source Windows tool whose capture modes include fullscreen, active window, region, scrolling capture and screen recording. The vendor says it needs no account and has no advertisements.

    It is a utility more than a gaming product, so there is no automatic highlight capture. If you mostly want fast captures and tasks that run after capture, it is the lightest option on this list.

    Checked on getsharex.com

    Visit ShareX

Which one fits

Start with where the footage comes from. If it is your own gameplay on a PC with a recent graphics card, try the vendor app for that card first, because it costs nothing extra to install.

If you want recorder-level control, OBS Studio or a paid Windows recorder fits. If the footage is a live broadcast, look at the stream clippers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Medal free?

Medal's homepage description says it is free to record, edit and share gaming clips on PC. The page we opened lists no paid tier, so check the vendor site for anything added after 18 November 2026.

Which Medal alternative needs no extra app on a gaming PC?

The NVIDIA App and AMD Software Adrenalin are driver companions most owners of those graphics cards already install. NVIDIA lists ShadowPlay among the App's features, and AMD says Adrenalin can capture in-game moments.

Can I record without an account?

ShareX says on its own page that it requires no account and is free forever. OBS Studio is free and open source recording software as well. Check each vendor's current terms before relying on that.

What if I want clips for TikTok or Shorts?

Eklipse and StreamLadder both turn Twitch, YouTube and Kick streams into vertical clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. They work from a stream, so they suit broadcasters more than players who only record their own matches.

Does a paid recorder make sense?

Bandicam and Action! both offer a free or trial route before payment. Neither opened page showed prices, so a paid recorder is worth it only if you need what the trial proves it does.
