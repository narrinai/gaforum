Substance 3D Painter Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Substance 3D Painter is Adobe's 3D texturing application, and this page treats it as the reference point for game artists who texture models. Adobe's product pages blocked our fetcher on 18 November 2026, so we make no claims about its price or feature list here.

The nine alternatives below are described only from their own vendor pages and cover painting on a mesh, procedural materials, baking and rendering, and sculpting. Several are free or open source, which is why teams compare them when licensing becomes a topic.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Substance 3D Painter

  • Cost models differ widely. Material Maker is open source with a donation link, while Mari lists subscriptions from EUR 78 per month, so teams price a switch by tool.
  • Some artists want a procedural workflow. Material Maker creates PBR materials using graphs of over 200 nodes, which is a different method from painting directly on a mesh.
  • Open and local tools appeal to studios with data rules. ArmorPaint says its AI models are published under open source licences and run locally.
  • Teams that already use Blender or Krita for modeling and painting prefer to keep the texturing step in the same free tool.
  • Pipelines that bake and render in one place look at Marmoset Toolbag, which describes baking, texturing and rendering in a single package.

The alternatives

  1. 1

    Marmoset Toolbag

    Baking, texturing and rendering in one package

    Free option
    Free trial

    Marmoset describes Toolbag as an all-in-one package for 3D artists to bake, texture and render, and says Toolbag 5 adds UDIMs, a Bevel shader and new painting tools. The free trial makes it easy to test.

    It is the natural pick when you want presentation renders alongside textures.

    Checked on marmoset.co

    Visit Marmoset Toolbag

  2. 2

    Blender

    Free modeling with texture painting in one app

    Free option
    Open source

    Blender's official manual documents installs on Linux, macOS, Windows and Steam. It saves a license fee and a round trip between programs, but it is a general tool, so expect to set up your own texturing workflow rather than follow a dedicated painter's layout.

    Checked on docs.blender.org, github.com

    Visit Blender

  3. 3

    3D-Coat

    Voxel and polygon sculpting with painting

    Free option
    Unknown

    3DCoat combines voxels and polygons for 3D model creation, and a 3DCoatTextura version is also offered. Professional, Educational and Floating licenses are available for Windows, macOS and Linux.

    It is a one-time purchase with paid update steps, which differs from subscription tools.

    Checked on 3dcoat.com

    Visit 3D-Coat

  4. 4

    Mari

    High-end 3D painting for large productions

    Free option
    Free trial

    The Foundry's Mari handles 3D texture and digital painting, and its page offers a free trial download. The price list is the highest in this group, so it suits studios already invested in film or VFX pipelines more than small game teams on a tight budget.

    Checked on foundry.com

    Visit Mari

  5. 5

    ArmorPaint

    Stand-alone PBR painting with local AI

    Free option
    Unknown

    ArmorPaint is stand-alone software for physically-based texture painting on 3D models, using node-based materials, fill layers and brush nodes. The homepage says AI models run locally and recommends at least 6 GB of video memory.

    It fits artists who want a painting-first interface without a subscription.

    Checked on armorpaint.org

    Visit ArmorPaint

  6. 6

    Material Maker

    Open source procedural materials

    Free option
    Open source

    Material Maker is described as the open source procedural materials authoring tool, with graphs of over 200 nodes for PBR materials and export for game engines. It is on itch.io and Steam.

    It replaces the material side of a painter, not hand painting on a mesh.

    Checked on materialmaker.org

    Visit Material Maker

  7. 7

    ZBrush

    Sculpting with texture tools included

    Free option
    Unknown

    Maxon's ZBrush page lists a dynamic brush system, base mesh creation and remeshing, and says subscriptions include ZBrush on desktop, iPad and Windows on Arm devices. It leads with sculpting, so choose it if high-poly detail comes before texturing in your pipeline.

    Checked on maxon.net

    Visit ZBrush

  8. 8

    Houdini

    Procedural look development

    Free option
    Free trial

    SideFX describes Houdini as 3D procedural software covering modeling, look development, lighting and rendering. It is a heavy move for texturing alone.

    It fits technical artists who already work procedurally and want materials and assets built from the same network.

    Checked on sidefx.com

    Visit Houdini

  9. 9

    Krita

    Free 2D painting for hand-painted textures

    Free option
    Open source

    Krita is described as a professional, free and open source painting program made by artists, with more than 25 interface languages. It paints in 2D, so you project or paint flat texture sheets rather than directly on a mesh.

    The download page lists Krita 5.3.4.1 released on 7 October 2026.

    Checked on krita.org

    Visit Krita

How to pick between them

Decide how your textures are made. Painted directly on a mesh points to ArmorPaint, 3D-Coat or Mari.

Built from graphs points to Material Maker or Houdini. If you also need renders, Marmoset Toolbag fits.

If your budget is zero, Blender, Krita, Material Maker and ArmorPaint are the places to start. Since we could not read Adobe's pages, compare the current Substance price and your trial results yourself before you leave it.

Frequently asked questions

Why does this page say nothing about Substance 3D Painter's price?

Adobe's pages returned an access error to our fetcher on 18 November 2026, so we did not state a price or feature we could not read. Check Adobe's current page for those details.

Which options are free?

Krita is free and open source, Material Maker is open source with a donation link, and Blender's manual covers the GPL. ArmorPaint shows no price on the pages opened and its AI models are under open source licences.

What is the cheapest paid alternative?

On the pages opened, 3D-Coat lists a licence at EUR 379 with paid update steps, and Mari's subscription starts at EUR 78 per month. Marmoset and ZBrush did not show prices in the captured text.

Can I paint textures without a sculpting tool?

Yes. ArmorPaint and Mari focus on painting, and Material Maker builds materials from nodes. Sculpting tools such as ZBrush and 3D-Coat are optional if your models arrive from elsewhere.

Do these tools run on Mac and Linux?

3D-Coat lists Windows, macOS and Linux, and Blender documents Linux, macOS and Windows. ZBrush lists desktop and iPad. Check the other vendors' system pages, since several did not state platforms on the pages opened.
