vMix Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

vMix is a live streaming and live production video mixer and switcher for Windows. Its homepage shows a purchase option from $50 per month and offers a free 60-day trial.

Because it only runs on Windows, a Mac-based team has to look elsewhere, and a small team may question the entry price. The nine options here include Mac apps, a free open source studio, cloud services and phone tools.

Compare them with the broader live production software ranking once you know which constraint is pushing you away from vMix.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond vMix

  • vMix is Windows software, so a Mac or a mixed fleet needs a different tool.
  • The homepage price of $50 per month is higher than several entry plans on this list, such as Ecamm Live at $16 per month.
  • OBS Studio offers a free license for teams that can accept doing more setup themselves.
  • Some productions want a vendor-run, cloud-based workflow rather than a PC under the desk, as LiveU describes.
  • Mobile reporters and remote contributors need an app that sends video from a phone, which Larix Broadcaster covers.

The alternatives

  1. 1

    OBS Studio

    Free, three operating systems

    Free option
    Open source

    OBS Studio is free and open source and supports Windows 10 and 11, macOS 13.0 or newer, and Linux. You gain freedom from Windows and from licensing cost.

    You give up the integrated production feel that vMix's homepage describes as a mixer and switcher. OBS builds scenes from sources such as window captures, browser windows, webcams and capture cards, so a small crew can do a lot with it if someone owns the setup.

    Checked on obsproject.com

    Visit OBS Studio

  2. 2

    Wirecast

    Mac and Windows with remote guests

    Free option
    Free trial

    Wirecast is live video streaming and broadcast software for Mac and Windows, with unlimited cameras, remote guests and multistreaming. It is the closest like-for-like commercial option that also covers Apple hardware.

    You gain Mac support. You give up price advantage, since Studio runs from $34 per month billed yearly or $59 month to month, which is not far from the $50 vMix purchase option shown on its homepage.

    Checked on wirecast.io

    Visit Wirecast

  3. 3

    Ecamm Live

    Mac-only, lower entry price

    Free option
    Free trial

    Ecamm Live is a live streaming and video production studio built for Mac and needs macOS 11.2 or newer. If your team's problem with vMix is that it needs Windows, this is the opposite choice: Apple only.

    You gain lower listed prices and a 14-day trial without a credit card. You give up Windows entirely, so a mixed office cannot standardize on it.

    Checked on ecamm.com, appg2.ecamm.com

    Visit Ecamm Live

  4. 4

    Vizrt

    Broadcast graphics and studio automation

    Free option
    Unknown

    Vizrt provides software-defined visual storytelling, including real-time 3D graphics, live production systems and studio automation tools. It targets broadcast teams more than small crews.

    You gain graphics and automation depth for larger productions. You give up published pricing, because the homepage says none, and you should expect to ask for a quote rather than start a trial like vMix's 60-day download.

    Checked on vizrt.com

    Visit Vizrt

  5. 5

    LiveU Studio

    Cloud-based production with field links

    Free option
    Unknown

    LiveU Studio appears on the LiveU homepage as the way to return live video from the studio to the field, and LiveU describes cloud-based, IP-bonded live video for contribution, production and distribution. You gain a workflow built for remote footage rather than a single PC.

    You give up a clear price and a feature list we could verify, so treat it as a product to request details on.

    Checked on liveu.tv

    Visit LiveU Studio

  6. 6

    Switcher Studio

    Simple multi-camera streaming

    Free option
    Free trial

    Switcher Studio is multi-camera live streaming software for switching cameras and streaming to several platforms. It is the gentler option for teams that find vMix more than they need.

    You gain a 14-day free trial and an uncomplicated pitch. You give up detail we could not read, including plan prices on its pricing page, so confirm costs and supported hardware before you rebuild a workflow around it.

    Checked on switcherstudio.com

    Visit Switcher Studio

  7. 7

    Larix Broadcaster

    Phone contribution

    Free option
    Free

    Larix Broadcaster is a free mobile broadcasting app from Softvelum for remote contribution streaming from Android and iOS devices. It is not a switcher, so it is a partner tool, not a replacement.

    You gain a free way to feed a remote camera. You give up production features entirely.

    Treat it as a source that feeds whatever you choose to replace vMix with.

    Checked on softvelum.com

    Visit Larix Broadcaster

  8. 8

    XSplit Broadcaster

    Windows streaming at a lower tier

    Free option
    Free

    XSplit Broadcaster is live streaming and recording software for games, podcasts and presentations, with scenes, sources and outputs in one workspace, and it runs on Windows. It suits teams that stay on Windows but want a cheaper tier.

    You gain a free plan and Premium at $14.99 per month or $59.99 per year. You give up some of the switcher feel of vMix.

    Checked on xsplit.com

    Visit XSplit Broadcaster

  9. 9

    Lightstream

    Browser-based studio

    Free option
    Free

    Lightstream is cloud-native streaming software that runs in the browser and needs no downloads, so the operating system stops mattering. The free tier includes 720p, a 10 hour limit per stream and up to 3 scenes with a Lightstream logo.

    You gain a Mac-friendly, install-free option. You give up the local power and multi-camera control that vMix users rely on for bigger shows.

    Checked on golightstream.com

    Visit Lightstream

How to pick between them

Ask what is wrong with vMix for you. If it is the operating system, choose OBS Studio, Wirecast, Ecamm Live or Lightstream depending on budget and Apple dependence.

If it is the price, start with OBS Studio, XSplit Broadcaster or Ecamm Live. If you need broadcast-level graphics or field links, request details from Vizrt or LiveU.

For phone-based sources, add Larix Broadcaster. Whatever you pick, rehearse a complete show on the new tool, because the 60-day vMix trial is a good reference point for how long you should be testing.

Frequently asked questions

Does vMix run on Mac?

The vMix homepage describes it as a video mixer and switcher for Windows. A Mac-based team can use Wirecast, Ecamm Live or OBS Studio, or run a browser tool like Lightstream.

How much does vMix cost?

The homepage shows a purchase option from $50 per month and a free 60-day trial download. We did not read every edition, so check the vendor's page for the version you need.

Which vMix alternative is free?

OBS Studio is free and open source, XSplit Broadcaster has a $0 tier, and Larix Broadcaster is a free mobile app. Lightstream has a free tier too, with a logo and limits on resolution and stream length.

Is there a vMix alternative for broadcasters?

Vizrt describes real-time 3D graphics, live production systems and studio automation. LiveU focuses on cloud-based, IP-bonded live video. Neither states pricing on its homepage, so budget for a sales conversation.

Can a hybrid Mac and Windows team standardize on one tool?

Wirecast supports both Mac and Windows, and OBS Studio supports Windows, macOS and Linux. Those are the two clearest single-tool answers among the pages we read.
