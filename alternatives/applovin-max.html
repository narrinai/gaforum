AppLovin MAX Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

AppLovin MAX is the mediation product in AppLovin's monetization offer, and its support center describes an SDK that you add with Gradle on Android or CocoaPods and Swift Package Manager on iOS, with minimum targets of Android minSdkVersion 24 and iOS 12.0. The docs also describe a MAX Ad Review service.

Studios compare it with other mediation layers and ad networks to diversify demand or to stay inside one engine's tooling. The eight entries below mix mediation platforms and networks, and each is described from its own page.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond AppLovin MAX

  • A studio that builds in Unity can integrate LevelPlay directly from the Unity Editor, which shortens setup compared with an external SDK.
  • Google AdMob states that all participating networks compete at the same priority and the highest-paying bid wins each impression, a rule some teams prefer to read before trusting an auction.
  • Ad networks such as Pangle and Chartboost give access to specific demand, like TikTok for Business advertisers or brand budgets via Chartboost Direct.
  • Teams that buy users as well as sell ad space may want one vendor for both, as Liftoff and Mintegral describe.
  • Most alternatives here do not publish fees, so a studio has to compare them by asking, which is a reason to keep more than one partner.

The alternatives

    Unity LevelPlay

    Unity games that want mediation in the editor

    Free option
    Unknown

    Unity LevelPlay is an ad mediation platform whose page promises higher revenue, new player acquisition and monetization control. Integration is available directly from the Unity Editor and through an SDK, which is the main draw for a Unity project.

    You gain editor-level setup; you give up neutrality if your game is built on another engine, and you must ask Unity what the platform costs.

    Google AdMob

    Apps that want Google demand and a free sign-up

    Free option
    Unknown

    AdMob is Google's mobile monetization platform for in-app ads, analytics and mediation tools. The page claims 1 million+ apps and 81% of the Android top 1000, and it says all participating networks compete at the same priority with the highest-paying bid winning each impression.

    You gain a familiar auction model and Google's reach; you give up an independent, game-specific mediation layer.

    Chartboost

    Publishers who want a monetization partner under LoopMe

    Free option
    Unknown

    Chartboost is described as an in-app monetization and programmatic advertising platform that LoopMe acquired in 2024. Its page talks about higher CPMs and fill rates and about Chartboost Direct, which lets publishers run brand demand in apps without changing their ad stack.

    You gain a brand-demand route; you give up a published fee, since the page states neither pricing nor headquarters.

    Pangle

    Free option
    Unknown

    Pangle is an in-app advertising network, described on its page as the ad network of TikTok for Business. The formats it names are rewarded video, interstitial, native, app open (beta), banner and playable ads.

    You gain a large social-platform advertiser pool and playable formats; you give up the mediation role of MAX, because this is a network you plug in rather than a layer that runs the auction.

    You gain an advertiser-side and publisher-side vendor in one; you give up a single-purpose mediation tool and any visible price.

    You gain a combined marketing and monetization stack; you give up focus, because monetization is one product among several and the page shows no fee.

How to pick between them

Separate mediation from networks first. LevelPlay and AdMob are mediation or monetization platforms you can run as the main layer; Pangle, Chartboost, Mintegral, Liftoff, InMobi and Moloco are demand sources you plug into one.

Choose the mediation layer by engine and by how much control you want over the auction, then add two or three networks and compare effective revenue per thousand impressions in your own reports. Ask each vendor for payment terms and minimums in writing before you integrate.

Frequently asked questions

Is an ad network the same as ad mediation?

Not exactly. A mediation platform such as Unity LevelPlay or Google AdMob manages several demand sources, while a network such as Pangle supplies advertisers. Many vendors offer both, so read each page for which role is meant.

Which alternative works best for a Unity game?

Unity LevelPlay says integration is available directly from the Unity Editor and an SDK. Other options on this list use SDKs, so setup effort differs. Pricing for LevelPlay is not stated on its page.

Can I run more than one of these at once?

Yes, that is the usual pattern. A mediation layer holds the auction, and networks like Pangle or Chartboost join it as demand. Check each vendor's documentation for supported adapter versions before you commit.

Do any of these publish their fees?

Few do. Moloco says pricing is performance-based and varies by product, goals and market, while AdMob offers an earnings calculator without stated fees. Most pages ask you to start an account or contact sales.

What does the AppLovin MAX SDK require?

The AppLovin support center says the Android SDK needs minSdkVersion 24 or above and the iOS SDK needs a deployment target of iOS 12.0 or above. It also notes the Android SDK collects the Google Advertising ID.
