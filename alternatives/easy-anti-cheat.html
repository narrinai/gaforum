Easy Anti-Cheat Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Easy Anti-Cheat is an anti-cheat service for online games and part of Epic Online Services; its vendor site says 200+ games and 35M+ players every month are protected. A studio that does not build on Epic's stack may want a vendor with no tie to a store or engine owner.

This page covers ten options: dedicated PC anti-cheat, regional services, security suites from larger companies, and mobile app protection that handles tampering rather than player behavior.

About the order: it reflects our own reading of each vendor's documentation, prices and license terms, weighed for the reader this page is written for. Reasons to leave Easy Anti-Cheat

  • Ecosystem fit: Easy Anti-Cheat belongs to Epic Online Services, and studios that avoid depending on one platform owner look for an independent vendor.
  • Pricing clarity: the licensing page lists free service tiers and paid Core, Fortified and Premier tiers, with no prices shown.
  • Regional coverage: studios publishing in Korea or China often need a vendor that is established in that market.
  • Mobile scope: Easy Anti-Cheat's pages focus on online games, while a mobile title may need app hardening against tampering.
  • Bundled protection: teams that already pay for piracy and leak protection sometimes want anti-cheat from the same supplier.

Choosing among these

Separate the two problems first. Behavior cheating on PC or console is the job of BattlEye, Denuvo Anti-Cheat, Anybrain or a regional vendor.

Tampering with a mobile client is the job of Appdome, Promon or Guardsquare, and these do not replace server-side detection. Next, check your launch region, since XIGNCODE3, nProtect GameGuard and ACE are tied to Asian markets.

Finally ask each vendor for terms in writing, because almost none publish prices. The anti-cheat category ranks them side by side.

Frequently asked questions

Which alternative is independent of any store or engine owner?

BattlEye describes itself as 100% independent and its imprint names BattlEye Innovations e.K. in Reutlingen, Germany. Easy Anti-Cheat is part of Epic Online Services, so independence from that ecosystem is the clearest reason to look at BattlEye.

Is there a free anti-cheat among these?

Easy Anti-Cheat itself lists free service tiers next to paid Core, Fortified and Premier tiers. None of the ten alternatives here shows a free tier on the opened pages, so expect to negotiate.

Can mobile app protection replace anti-cheat?

Not fully. Appdome, Promon and Guardsquare harden the app against tampering, while anti-cheat services detect cheating behavior and manage bans. Many mobile studios combine both, depending on how their game verifies actions on the server.

Do I need a regional vendor for Asia?

Possibly. XIGNCODE3, nProtect GameGuard and Anti-Cheat Expert are rooted in Korean and Chinese markets. If your player base there is small, a global vendor may be simpler; if it is large, ask what local support and certifications each one offers.

What should I ask a vendor before signing?

Ask which platforms the client supports, how appeals and false bans are handled, how quickly new cheats get a response, and what the contract costs per title or per player. Prices are unpublished almost everywhere in this list, so written terms matter.
