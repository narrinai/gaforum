Wirecast Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Wirecast is live video streaming and broadcast software for Mac and Windows, with unlimited cameras, remote guests and multistreaming. Its pricing page lists Studio from $34 per month billed yearly ($408 per year) or $59 month to month, and Pro from $42 per month billed yearly.

Teams that want to spend less, or that want hardware, a Mac-native app or a free license, have several routes. The nine tools below each answer a different reason for leaving, and the live production software ranking puts them in context.

Why this order: we opened every vendor's own pages on the review date and placed the products by fit, not by popularity or payment. When to replace Wirecast

  • Wirecast Studio costs $408 per year on annual billing or $59 per month without a commitment, which adds up across several seats.
  • A free tool such as OBS Studio covers basic switching and recording at no license cost.
  • Teams with fixed studios may prefer a hardware switcher like Blackmagic ATEM over a computer-bound application.
  • Mac-only shops may like a tool built for macOS, as Ecamm Live is.
  • Productions that connect many cameras over a network can build around NDI Tools instead of a single application.

Our picks

  1. 1

    vMix

    Windows production on a different license

    Free option
    Free trial

    vMix is a live streaming and live production video mixer and switcher for Windows. It is the most direct competitor if your shows already live on a Windows PC.

    You gain a 60-day trial, which is longer than a typical demo window. You give up the Mac, since vMix is Windows only, and the homepage shows purchase from $50 per month, so compare that number with Wirecast's own plans before assuming it is cheaper.

    Checked on vmix.com

    Visit vMix

  2. 2

    OBS Studio

    A free license

    Free option
    Open source

    OBS Studio is free and open source for video recording and live streaming on Windows 10 and 11, macOS 13.0 or newer, and Linux. You gain zero license cost and a third operating system.

    You give up a commercial vendor to call. Its scenes are built from multiple sources including window captures, browser windows, webcams and capture cards, which suits small crews comfortable assembling their own setup.

    Checked on obsproject.com

    Visit OBS Studio

  3. 3

    Vizrt

    Broadcast-grade graphics and studios

    Free option
    Unknown

    Vizrt provides software-defined visual storytelling, including real-time 3D graphics, live production systems and studio automation tools. It sits above Wirecast in ambition and aims at broadcasters.

    You gain graphics and automation breadth. You give up transparent pricing, since the homepage says nothing about cost, and you should expect a sales conversation rather than a monthly plan you can start at once.

    Checked on vizrt.com

    Visit Vizrt

  4. 4

    Ecamm Live

    Mac-native studio

    Free option
    Free trial

    Ecamm Live is a live streaming and video production studio built for Mac and needs macOS 11.2 or newer. Pick it if your team is all on Apple hardware and wants lower entry pricing than Wirecast Studio.

    You gain a Mac-first design. You give up Windows support and the unlimited-camera, remote-guest feature set that Wirecast describes on its own homepage.

    Checked on ecamm.com, appg2.ecamm.com

    Visit Ecamm Live

  5. 5

    LiveU Studio

    Cloud production tied to field video

    Free option
    Unknown

    LiveU Studio is listed on the LiveU homepage with the line return live video from the studio to the field, and LiveU describes cloud-based, IP-bonded live video for contribution, production and distribution. It fits teams whose footage arrives over cellular links.

    You gain a connected field-to-cloud flow. You give up a published price, which the homepage omits, so ask for a quote before comparing with Wirecast.

    Checked on liveu.tv

    Visit LiveU Studio

  6. 6

    Switcher Studio

    Multi-camera shows from phones and tablets

    Free option
    Free trial

    Switcher Studio is multi-camera live streaming software that lets users switch cameras and stream to several platforms. It is a lighter alternative for teams building shows from simple cameras.

    You gain a quick start with a 14-day free trial. You give up detail we cannot confirm, such as plan prices and platform lists, because the pricing page text we read did not carry amounts.

    Check them directly before budgeting.

    Checked on switcherstudio.com

    Visit Switcher Studio

  7. 7

    NDI Tools

    Network video routing

    Free option
    Free

    NDI Tools is a free set of tools and plugins for setting up, testing and managing NDI video streams on Windows and macOS, including Discovery, Studio Monitor and NDI Free Audio. It is not a switcher by itself.

    You gain a way to move video between machines on a network without extra hardware, which can let a lighter switcher do the job. You give up an all-in-one program.

    Checked on ndi.video

    Visit NDI Tools

  8. 8

    Blackmagic ATEM

    Hardware switching for fixed studios

    Free option
    Unknown

    Blackmagic ATEM Constellation switchers range from 1 M/E with 10 inputs up to a 4 M/E 4K Plus model with 80 inputs, and the free ATEM Software Control is included. You gain dedicated hardware and a physical panel option, the ATEM Advanced Panel.

    You give up the subscription model altogether in favor of a hardware purchase, and prices were not on the page we opened.

    Checked on blackmagicdesign.com

    Visit Blackmagic ATEM

  9. 9

    Lightstream

    Browser-based streaming

    Free option
    Free

    Lightstream is a cloud-native streaming tool that runs in the browser and needs no downloads. It can simulcast to up to five destinations at once, including custom RTMP.

    The free tier is limited to 720p, 10 hours per stream and 3 scenes with a logo. You gain a paid tier at $9 per month on annual billing.

    You give up the multi-camera and remote-guest depth that Wirecast advertises.

    Checked on golightstream.com

    Visit Lightstream

Making the call

Match the tool to where your show physically runs. A Windows production PC points to vMix or OBS Studio.

An all-Apple crew points to Ecamm Live. A permanent studio with a control room points to Blackmagic ATEM, with NDI Tools if you route video over a network.

Broadcast newsrooms should speak to Vizrt or LiveU. Small teams who only need a quick multi-camera show can try Switcher Studio, or Lightstream if a browser is enough.

Use trials where offered and run one real event before you cancel Wirecast.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Wirecast cost?

Its pricing page lists Studio from $34 per month billed yearly ($408 per year) or $59 month to month, and Pro from $42 per month billed yearly. The page also says the free trial includes every feature.

What is the cheapest route away from Wirecast?

OBS Studio is free and open source. Lightstream has a free tier with a logo, and Ecamm Live starts at $16 per month on its pricing page. Cheapest depends on whether you count hardware and your own setup time.

Which alternatives work on a Mac?

OBS Studio supports macOS 13.0 or newer, Ecamm Live is built for Mac, and NDI Tools runs on Windows and macOS. Lightstream runs in a browser. vMix is Windows only.

Do I need hardware to replace Wirecast?

No. Software options such as vMix and OBS Studio run on a standard PC. Blackmagic ATEM is the hardware route and suits fixed rooms where physical buttons help operators.

Can I test before leaving Wirecast?

Yes for most options: vMix offers a 60-day trial, Ecamm Live 14 days, and Switcher Studio 14 days. OBS Studio is free to install. Run a full-length event before you commit.
