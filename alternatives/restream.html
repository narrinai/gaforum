Restream Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Restream is a live video platform that lets users create videos and multistream them to multiple channels at once; its pricing page says to start multistreaming for free or choose a plan, and the company behind it is Restream, Inc. We could not capture plan prices from the page, so this list focuses on what the alternatives publish. It is for people who want to compare cost, who want to send one stream to many destinations from a tool they control, or who need more than multistreaming.

The multistreaming platform ranking shows how these fit together.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Restream

  • Price transparency differs: OneStream Live and Castr publish plan prices that you can set against your current bill.
  • Some free desktop tools, such as Prism Live Studio, include multistreaming in the software itself.
  • A tool that also records and edits, like Riverside, can replace several subscriptions at once.
  • Public-sector and church streams may want a platform built for unlimited destinations, which Switchboard Live describes.
  • Teams that already own a Mac studio may prefer a local application over a web service.

How to pick between them

Count your destinations and your guests. For two destinations and a solo show, a free option such as OneStream Live's Free plan or Prism Live Studio may be enough.

If guests join your show, look at StreamYard or Riverside. If you also need hosting, VOD or delivery, Castr is the fit.

Institutions with many partners should talk to Switchboard Live. Whatever you choose, run a private test stream to every destination first, because platform-side settings, not the tool, cause most failed multistreams.

Frequently asked questions

What does Restream do?

Restream describes itself as a simple live video platform for creating videos and multistreaming them to your favorite channels. Its pricing page says you can start multistreaming for free or pick a plan. We could not capture the plan prices, so check them on the vendor site.

Which Restream alternative has a free plan?

OneStream Live lists a free plan at $0 with 2 multistreaming destinations and 720p, StreamYard has a free plan, Lightstream has a free tier with a logo, and Prism Live Studio is free software. Riverside also has a free plan with a watermark.

Do I need software on my computer?

Not always. StreamYard, Lightstream, Be.Live and OneStream Live work in a browser. Streamlabs Desktop, Ecamm Live and Prism Live Studio are applications you install.

Which option suits a church or a public body?

Switchboard Live describes itself as government-approved and aimed at public sector, houses of worship, entertainment and political campaigns. Ask for pricing, because its figures were not in the text we captured.

Can I stream from an external encoder?

OneStream Live says it accepts external encoders via RTMP, RTMPS, SRT and WHIP, and Lightstream mentions custom RTMP destinations. Check each vendor's documentation for your encoder before you plan an event.
