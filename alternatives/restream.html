Restream Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Restream is a live video platform that lets users create videos and multistream them to multiple channels at once; its pricing page says to start multistreaming for free or choose a plan, and the company behind it is Restream, Inc. We could not capture plan prices from the page, so this list focuses on what the alternatives publish. It is for people who want to compare cost, who want to send one stream to many destinations from a tool they control, or who need more than multistreaming.

The multistreaming platform ranking shows how these fit together.

Ordering: editorial, based on what each vendor publishes about pricing, licensing and features as of the date above. What pushes teams away from Restream

  • Price transparency differs: OneStream Live and Castr publish plan prices that you can set against your current bill.
  • Some free desktop tools, such as Prism Live Studio, include multistreaming in the software itself.
  • A tool that also records and edits, like Riverside, can replace several subscriptions at once.
  • Public-sector and church streams may want a platform built for unlimited destinations, which Switchboard Live describes.
  • Teams that already own a Mac studio may prefer a local application over a web service.

What to use instead

  1. 1

    Castr

    Hosting plus multistreaming

    Free option
    Free trial

    Castr combines stream hosting, multistreaming, VOD and OTT in one platform with multi-CDN delivery. Pick it when you want streaming infrastructure as well as a way to reach several destinations.

    You gain hosting and video on demand in one account. You give up the lighter, creator-first feel, as plans start at $16.67 per month and the product aims at broader video delivery.

    A 7-day Starter trial lets you try it first.

    Visit Castr

  2. 2

    StreamYard

    Browser studio with guests

    Free option
    Free

    StreamYard is a browser-based studio that streams to Facebook, YouTube and other platforms, and says it is trusted by 12,000,000+ creators. You gain a place to host guests and record locally, with 2 hours per month on the free plan.

    You give up a desktop app. The individual plan costs $18.99 per month billed monthly, or $14.99 per month billed yearly, with a 7-day free trial.

    Checked on streamyard.com

    Visit StreamYard

  3. 3

    OneStream Live

    Published tiers and many destinations

    Free option
    Free plan

    OneStream Live is a cloud-based platform that sends one stream to several destinations from a browser, an encoder or pre-recorded videos. Its free plan includes 2 destinations and 720p.

    You gain a clear ladder of plans from $15 to $99 per month and support for encoders using RTMP, RTMPS, SRT and WHIP. You give up simplicity if you only need two destinations.

    The pricing page does not name its currency.

    Checked on onestream.live

    Visit OneStream Live

  4. 4

    Switchboard Live

    Unlimited destinations for institutions

    Free option
    Unknown

    Switchboard Live calls itself a government-approved live streaming management platform, and Switchboard Connect streams unlimited minutes to unlimited destinations according to its homepage. Features include PartnerShare, scheduled events and event analytics.

    You gain a service aimed at public sector, houses of worship and campaigns. You give up an individual-creator experience, and we could not read its plan prices, so request a quote before comparing costs.

    Checked on switchboard.live

    Visit Switchboard Live

  5. 5

    Be.Live

    Branding and viewer engagement

    Free option
    Free trial

    Be.Live is browser-based live streaming software for multistreaming, custom branding and engagement tools, and it offers an AI Comment Assistant that can feature comments on screen. You gain on-screen engagement features.

    You give up price clarity, since its home page showed only discount banners and we did not open its pricing page. The 14-day free trial needs no credit card, so you can judge value yourself.

    Checked on be.live

    Visit Be.Live

  6. 6

    Lightstream

    Browser streaming with simulcast

    Free option
    Free

    Lightstream runs in the browser and can simulcast to up to five destinations at once, including custom RTMP. It also offers cloud capture for Xbox and PlayStation.

    The free tier is capped at 720p and 10 hours per stream with a logo, while Premium removes the logo and adds 1080p. You gain console capture without a PC.

    You give up high destination counts beyond five.

    Checked on golightstream.com

    Visit Lightstream

  7. 7

    Streamlabs Desktop

    Desktop software with creator extras

    Free option
    Unknown

    Streamlabs offers Streamlabs Desktop alongside alerts, overlays, tipping and merch for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook. You gain a one-account setup for a streamer who also wants tipping and overlays.

    You give up information, because the Desktop product page renders with JavaScript and we could not record its plans. Its pricing page does state that the company takes zero cut of your donations.

    Checked on streamlabs.com

    Visit Streamlabs Desktop

  8. 8

    Ecamm Live

    A Mac-based studio

    Free option
    Free trial

    Ecamm Live is a live streaming and video production studio built for Mac that needs macOS 11.2 or newer. It is a way to bring the production and the distribution into one app on your own computer.

    You gain a Mac studio and a 14-day trial with no credit card. You give up Windows and browser-only use, and the pages we read do not describe how many destinations it supports.

    Checked on ecamm.com, appg2.ecamm.com

    Visit Ecamm Live

  9. 9

    Prism Live Studio

    Free multistreaming from the desktop

    Free option
    Free

    PRISM Live Studio is free live streaming and recording software that provides multi-platform streaming and video editing. Its homepage lists simultaneous streaming to live platforms and Max Multistream.

    It runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and Apple Silicon Macs on Monterey 12.3 or higher. You gain a no-cost desktop route.

    You give up a hosted service, so your own computer and connection carry the load.

    Checked on prismlive.com

    Visit Prism Live Studio

  10. 10

    Riverside

    Recording and streaming together

    Free option
    Free plan

    Riverside is an AI-powered platform to record, edit, repurpose, stream and publish podcasts, interviews and webinars. Its free plan has 720p video, a watermark and 2 hours of multi-track recording, and Pro is billed $288 annually with up to 4K video.

    You gain recording and editing beside the stream. You give up a focused multistreaming product, and its pricing page showed a staging notice.

    Checked on riverside.com

    Visit Riverside

Which one fits

Count your destinations and your guests. For two destinations and a solo show, a free option such as OneStream Live's Free plan or Prism Live Studio may be enough.

If guests join your show, look at StreamYard or Riverside. If you also need hosting, VOD or delivery, Castr is the fit.

Institutions with many partners should talk to Switchboard Live. Whatever you choose, run a private test stream to every destination first, because platform-side settings, not the tool, cause most failed multistreams.

Frequently asked questions

What does Restream do?

Restream describes itself as a simple live video platform for creating videos and multistreaming them to your favorite channels. Its pricing page says you can start multistreaming for free or pick a plan. We could not capture the plan prices, so check them on the vendor site.

Which Restream alternative has a free plan?

OneStream Live lists a free plan at $0 with 2 multistreaming destinations and 720p, StreamYard has a free plan, Lightstream has a free tier with a logo, and Prism Live Studio is free software. Riverside also has a free plan with a watermark.

Do I need software on my computer?

Not always. StreamYard, Lightstream, Be.Live and OneStream Live work in a browser. Streamlabs Desktop, Ecamm Live and Prism Live Studio are applications you install.

Which option suits a church or a public body?

Switchboard Live describes itself as government-approved and aimed at public sector, houses of worship, entertainment and political campaigns. Ask for pricing, because its figures were not in the text we captured.

Can I stream from an external encoder?

OneStream Live says it accepts external encoders via RTMP, RTMPS, SRT and WHIP, and Lightstream mentions custom RTMP destinations. Check each vendor's documentation for your encoder before you plan an event.
