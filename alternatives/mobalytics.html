Mobalytics Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Mobalytics is a game stats and coaching site, and players often compare it with narrower tools that focus on one title or one skill. Its website returned HTTP 403 to our research fetcher, so this page does not claim anything about Mobalytics features or prices; each alternative is described only from its own pages.

The picks run from League of Legends builds and match history to CS2 performance reports, Dota 2 analytics, aim training and a coaching marketplace. They are grouped by the job they do, so you can replace the part of Mobalytics you actually use.

The esports analytics and coaching software ranking gives a wider view.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Mobalytics

  • A player who only needs one game may prefer a site built around that title, such as STRATZ for Dota 2 or Scope.gg for CS2.
  • Players who want an in-game overlay can look at Blitz, which offers overlays plus meta stats and tier lists.
  • Those who want human coaching can use Metafy, where coaches and creators sell guides, coaching and events.
  • Improving raw aim is a different skill from reading stats, and Aim Lab is built only for that.
  • Several rivals publish a free tier plainly, which makes a switch low risk.

How to pick between them

Name the job first. Looking up matches in League of Legends points to OP.GG or U.GG.

Playing with an overlay points to Blitz. CS2 reviews point to Leetify or Scope.gg, and Dota 2 data to STRATZ.

If your problem is mechanical skill, try Aim Lab. If you want feedback from a person, browse Metafy.

Most of these are free to try, so use two for a week and keep the one you actually open after games.

Frequently asked questions

Why does this page not describe Mobalytics features?

Its website returned HTTP 403 to our fetcher, so we could not verify anything it offers or charges. We chose to describe only the alternatives from pages we opened.

Which tools are free to start?

Blitz, U.GG, STRATZ, Scope.gg and Aim Lab each describe a free way to use them, with optional paid tiers on several. Leetify's prices could not be read, and Metafy depends on what each coach charges.

I only play CS2. What fits?

Leetify and Scope.gg both focus on Counter-Strike 2 analytics. Leetify emphasizes match reports and highlights, while Scope.gg emphasizes stats beyond the scoreboard and lists a demo viewer and API.

Can any of these replace a human coach?

Metafy lets you book coaches and buy guides from creators. The stats sites and Aim Lab give data and practice, not personal feedback.

Is there a data source for developers?

STRATZ offers a GraphQL API, and Scope.gg lists an API in its navigation. Both are worth reading for rate limits and terms before you build on them.
