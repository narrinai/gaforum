BattlEye Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

BattlEye is an independent PC game anti-cheat service that detects cheats and permanently bans cheaters, operated by BattlEye Innovations e.K. in Reutlingen, Germany. It is a common choice in competitive shooters and survival games.

If you are shipping that kind of title and want a second opinion, nine alternatives follow, from the free-to-start Easy Anti-Cheat to regional services and mobile hardening kits. Where an option only fits part of the problem, the note says so.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond BattlEye

  • Opaque terms: BattlEye does not publish prices on the opened pages and points to a contact page.
  • Tier structure: Easy Anti-Cheat lists a free service tier, which attracts small teams that want to test before paying.
  • Cross-platform needs: a shooter that adds console or mobile builds may want a vendor that states coverage across all three.
  • Supplier consolidation: publishers that already buy piracy and leak protection may prefer one security vendor.
  • Regional launches: games aimed at Korea or China sometimes need an anti-cheat vendor local to those markets.

How to pick between them

Decide platform first. A PC-only shooter has the shortest list: Easy Anti-Cheat for a free-to-start path, Denuvo Anti-Cheat or Irdeto if you also want leak protection, Anybrain if you expect console builds.

Add a regional vendor only if a large share of players sits in Korea or China. Appdome and Promon belong on the list only when a mobile client exists.

Whatever you pick, get a written answer on false-ban appeals and update speed, then see the anti-cheat ranking.

Frequently asked questions

What is the closest alternative to BattlEye for a PC shooter?

Easy Anti-Cheat is the closest like-for-like option: both target online PC games and both issue bans. It differs in ownership, since it is part of Epic Online Services, and in having a free service tier alongside paid Core, Fortified and Premier tiers.

Does BattlEye publish prices?

Not on the pages opened for this page; the site offers a contact page instead. The same is true for most alternatives here, so comparing cost means requesting quotes from two or three vendors with the same player-count assumptions.

Is mobile hardening enough for a survival game?

No, if the cheating happens on PC. Appdome, Promon and Guardsquare-style tools protect mobile app binaries, not a PC client. They are an extra layer when a mobile companion exists.

Which vendors state console coverage?

Anybrain says its anti-cheat works across PC, console and mobile, and WELLBIA lists PC, mobile and console protection. Confirm the exact consoles and certification process directly, because the opened pages do not list them.

Can I run two anti-cheat systems together?

Running two kernel-level or driver-based tools in one client can cause conflicts and is something to settle with each vendor before you try it. A more common pattern is one anti-cheat plus separate server-side checks of your own.
