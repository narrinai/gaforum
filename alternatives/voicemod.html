Voicemod Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Voicemod is a real-time AI voice changer and soundboard for PC and Mac. It is free to download, some functions and content sit behind a Voicemod PRO license or in-app purchases, and its home page advertises more than 200 voices.

People who look past it usually want one narrower job done well: mixing several audio sources, removing background noise, routing a headset into a stream, or generating a synthetic voice. The eight tools below are grouped by that job, so the right replacement depends on what you actually use Voicemod for.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Voicemod

  • The Voicemod terms of use say some functions and content need a PRO license or in-app purchases, so streamers who want a cost they can predict compare it against donationware and free tools.
  • A voice changer and a mixer are different jobs. Voicemeeter and Elgato Wave Link are built around routing and mixing audio sources, which Voicemod's voice-effect focus does not cover on its own.
  • Noise removal is its own category. Krisp and NVIDIA Broadcast describe noise cancellation or removal as a core function, which matters more than a funny voice for a call-heavy day.
  • Teams that need cloned or synthetic voices for production work look at ElevenLabs or Respeecher, which are built around voice models and APIs rather than a live effects bar.
  • Voicemod runs on PC and Mac. Anyone who needs Windows-specific tooling, or a hardware-linked tool from a headset or microphone maker, ends up on SteelSeries Sonar or Wave Link instead.

The alternatives

  1. 1

    Voicemeeter

    Best for: Free virtual audio mixing on Windows

    Pricing
    Donationware: free to use with all functions except optional features, with a license invitation after 30 days and five suggested amounts in the webshop.
    Free option
    Free

    Voicemeeter is a mixer with a virtual audio device, not a voice changer, and that is the point. If your Voicemod setup mostly exists to route a microphone, a game and music into a stream, Voicemeeter does that routing with a pay-what-you-want model.

    You give up the preset voice library and gain a lot of control over which source goes where.

    Sources checked
    • Voicemeeter is an audio mixer application with a virtual audio device used to mix and manage audio sources. vb-audio.com/Voicemeeter
    • Voicemeeter is free to use with all functions available except optional features, under a donationware model. vb-audio.com/Voicemeeter
    • The licensing page says the webshop proposes five different amounts for the donationware license. vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

    Visit Voicemeeter

  2. 2

    Elgato Wave Link

    Best for: Creators routing audio on Windows or Mac

    Pricing
    Free for Windows and Mac per the Wave Link 3.5 text; some effects plug-ins are sold separately on the Elgato Marketplace and are Windows only.
    Free option
    Free

    Wave Link covers the routing side of a stream setup and adds a built-in soundboard in version 3.5, which overlaps with Voicemod's soundboard. Effects use VST3 on Windows and AU on macOS, so you bring your own plug-ins rather than a bundled voice catalog.

    Pick it if your pain point is balancing sources, not changing how you sound.

    Sources checked
    • Wave Link is audio software built for creators, offering broadcast-level routing and effects. www.elgato.com/us/en/s/wave-link-app
    • Wave Link 3.5 adds a built-in soundboard, Smart Switch, Audio Flow and Wave Cast for dual-PC audio, and the page says it is free for Windows and Mac. www.elgato.com/us/en/s/wave-link-app
    • The page says effects use VST3 on Windows and AU on macOS. www.elgato.com/us/en/s/wave-link-app

    Visit Elgato Wave Link

    SteelSeries Sonar

    Best for: Per-game sound tuning on Windows 10

    Pricing
    A free download button for Sonar LP is shown on the vendor page.
    Free option
    Free

    Sonar is described as a way to raise or lower specific in-game sounds and shape the audio spectrum. That is a listening-side tool, where Voicemod works on what others hear from you.

    The page lists Windows 10 (RS5+) only, so Mac users are out. It suits players who care about hearing footsteps more than sounding like someone else.

    Sources checked
    • The Sonar page describes it as a way to amplify or reduce specific in-game sounds and fine-tune the audio spectrum. steelseries.com/gg/sonar
    • The page lists Windows 10 (RS5+) as the platform. steelseries.com/gg/sonar
    • The page shows a free download button for Sonar LP. steelseries.com/gg/sonar

    Visit SteelSeries Sonar

    Choose it when the job is clean speech in a work call or a collaborative stream, and be ready for per-user pricing instead of a one-time license.

    There is no voice-character library, so it replaces Voicemod only if you used it as a filter, not as a costume.

How to pick between them

Start with the single task you used Voicemod for last week. If it was costumes and soundboard clips, MorphVOX or the Wave Link soundboard are the nearest swaps.

If it was juggling a mic, game and music, move to Voicemeeter or Wave Link. If viewers complained about background noise, Krisp or NVIDIA Broadcast solve that better.

If the voice is for finished content, not a live session, look at ElevenLabs or Respeecher and budget by credits or plan tier.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free replacement for Voicemod?

Several tools here cost nothing to start. Voicemeeter is donationware, Wave Link is free for Windows and Mac per its 3.5 page text, and SteelSeries Sonar shows a free download. None of them copies Voicemod's voice library, so free here means a different tool for a different part of the job.

Which Voicemod alternative works on Mac?

Elgato Wave Link lists macOS with AU effects, and MorphVOX Mac is sold for Macintosh computers. Voicemeeter and SteelSeries Sonar are Windows tools per their pages, and NVIDIA Broadcast lists Windows only. Voicemod itself covers PC and Mac, so Mac users have fewer direct swaps.

Can I mix audio and change my voice with one program?

Not with a single tool in this list. Mixers such as Voicemeeter and Wave Link route sources, while MorphVOX changes the voice. Many streamers chain them, sending the voice changer's output into the mixer as one more input.

Are AI voice services a live voice changer?

ElevenLabs and Respeecher are described by their vendors around voice cloning, text to speech and production work. They are credit or plan based services, and their pages do not present them as a drop-in live effect for a game chat, so treat them as content tools.

What should I check before paying for any of these?

Read the current pricing page, since several vendors show first-month discounts or leave the price off the home page. MorphVOX and NVIDIA Broadcast did not state prices on the pages we opened, so confirm the cost directly.
