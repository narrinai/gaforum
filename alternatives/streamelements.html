StreamElements Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

StreamElements is a cloud-based platform that bundles overlays, alerts, a chatbot, tipping and merchandise tools for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook Gaming, and its SE.Live plugin works with OBS Studio. Streamers look for alternatives when they want only part of that bundle, want to split bot and overlays across vendors, or want a different way to earn from viewers.

The eight tools below replace StreamElements in whole or in part: two overlay suites, a lighting-driven app, two chat bots, and three ways to monetize an audience.

About the order: it reflects our own reading of each vendor's documentation, prices and license terms, weighed for the reader this page is written for. Reasons to leave StreamElements

  • You want a chat bot that is free and cloud hosted on its own, without taking the rest of a platform along with it.
  • A tool that controls lights and devices from viewer events appeals more than static overlays and alerts.
  • You would rather buy designed overlays and alerts as a pass or a pack than build them in a dashboard.
  • Your income plan leans on a merch shop or memberships instead of tips and alerts.
  • A fundraising or charity-stream setup needs tools built around donors, not around a general stream dashboard.

The options, in order

  1. 1

    Streamlabs Desktop

    The closest all-in-one rival

    Free option
    Unknown

    Streamlabs is the direct competitor: it offers Streamlabs Desktop, alerts, overlays, tipping and merch for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook. Its pricing page states the company takes zero cut of your donations.

    That makes it the least disruptive swap, because the feature list lines up closely. We could not read the Streamlabs Desktop product page in full, so plan details need a check on the vendor site.

    Visit Streamlabs Desktop

  2. 2

    OWN3D

    Designed overlays and alerts, plus free tools

    Free option
    Free

    OWN3D is run by StreamTV Media GmbH in Austria and says it was founded in 2017. With an active Stream Pass you get more than 500 overlays, 12,000 stream designs and 500 alert designs in its Scene Builder, and the chat widget, chatbot, donation page and goal bars are free to OWN3D Pro users.

    Choose it if design assets matter more to you than a bot.

    Checked on own3d.tv

    Visit OWN3D

  3. 3

    Lumia Stream

    Chat, alerts and viewer-controlled lights

    Free option
    Free

    Lumia Stream combines multistream chat, alerts, OBS overlays, a loyalty system and lights or devices that viewers can trigger. It runs on Windows, Mac and Linux, and lists Stream Deck and Touch Portal plugins.

    It is the odd one here because the physical room is part of the show. The free plan lets you test it; Premium renews automatically unless you cancel.

    Checked on lumiastream.com

    Visit Lumia Stream

  4. 4

    Nightbot

    A dedicated chat bot

    Free option
    Unknown

    Nightbot is a chat bot for Twitch, YouTube and SOOP that automates chat with moderation, commands and timers. Its documentation covers commands, variables, spam protection and a Discord integration.

    If StreamElements is mainly your bot, Nightbot is the focused version of it. You would then pick overlays and alerts somewhere else, so this swap adds a second vendor.

    Checked on docs.nightbot.tv, nightbot.tv

    Visit Nightbot

  5. 5

    Fossabot

    A free cloud bot with Nightbot import

    Free option
    Free

    Fossabot is described by its maker as free and cloud hosted, with chat moderation, commands, timers and alerts for Twitch, YouTube, Kick and X. It can import commands, timers, spam filters and blocked terms from Nightbot, which helps if you already moved a bot once.

    It covers the chat side only, so overlays and tipping still need another tool.

    Checked on fossabot.com

    Visit Fossabot

  6. 6

    Streamloots

    Viewer-triggered cards and subscriptions

    Free option
    Free

    Streamloots is a monetization platform built around interactive cards that viewers trigger, plus subscriptions, auctions, a marathon timer and tips. The vendor says payouts run twice a month, on the 1st and 15th.

    It is a revenue idea more than an overlay suite. The vendor page we opened carried a 2024 copyright line, so check that the service is current before you commit.

    Checked on streamloots.com

    Visit Streamloots

  7. 7

    Tiltify

    Charity streams and fundraisers

    Free option
    Unknown

    Tiltify describes itself as made for fundraisers, with tools to engage donors and raise more for charities. Most of its pages are rendered with JavaScript, so we could not read feature details or plan amounts.

    We list it for charity streams and marathons, where a fundraising tool fits better than a general stream dashboard. Confirm fees and features on its site.

    Checked on tiltify.com

    Visit Tiltify

  8. 8

    Fourthwall

    Merch, memberships and branded shops

    Free option
    Free plan

    Fourthwall describes itself as one platform for custom products and branded shops. Its Free plan is $0 a month and includes monthly memberships, with an upgrade to Pro at any time.

    The home page lists fulfillment and shipping, sales channels and members-only videos. It replaces only the merchandise side of StreamElements, so keep a bot and overlays elsewhere.

    Checked on fourthwall.com

    Visit Fourthwall

Choosing among these

Split the StreamElements bundle into parts and decide which one you actually use. If it is the chat bot, compare Nightbot and Fossabot.

If it is overlays and alerts, look at Streamlabs or OWN3D. If it is income from viewers, Fourthwall, Streamloots and Tiltify each cover a different model.

Frequently asked questions

What does StreamElements include?

Its home page lists overlays, tipping, chatbot, alerts and merchandise for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook Gaming. It also lists SE.Live, a plugin for OBS Studio, and Ground Control, a multistreaming companion app. The home page did not show a price.

Which alternative is the nearest full replacement?

Streamlabs lines up most closely, with its desktop software, alerts, overlays, tipping and merch. OWN3D is the other suite here, and it leans toward designed assets. Neither page we opened gave full plan prices, so compare them on the vendor sites.

Can I keep my chat commands when I move bots?

Fossabot says it can import commands, timers, spam filters and blocked terms from Nightbot. Moving from StreamElements' bot would be a manual rebuild unless a vendor states otherwise, so list your commands before you switch.

Is there a free option with lights or smart devices?

Lumia Stream lists a free plan with a download button, and its Premium plan is $5.99 a month, $32.99 every 6 months or $59.99 a year. Viewer-controlled lights and devices are central to what it sells.

Where can I sell merch without a bot or overlay?

Fourthwall is a commerce platform for custom products, branded shops, digital products and memberships. Its Free plan is $0 a month. It does not handle alerts or chat, so it pairs with a separate bot.
