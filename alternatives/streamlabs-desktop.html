Streamlabs Desktop Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Streamlabs Desktop is the streaming software from Streamlabs, a company that also sells alerts, overlays, tipping and merch for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook. Gaming streamers who leave usually want a lighter or freer encoder, a browser-based setup that spares the gaming PC, or their overlays from a different company.

The nine alternatives below cover free desktop software, a cloud studio, mobile and multi-camera apps, and driver tools. We could not read the Streamlabs Desktop product page in full, so we describe it only through the company's main pages.

Reasons to leave Streamlabs Desktop

  • You want a free and open source encoder with no company layer on top, which is what OBS Studio describes itself as.
  • You would like streaming and multistreaming from one free app, without paying for an add-on tier.
  • Your gaming PC is busy enough that a browser-based studio that runs in the cloud looks attractive.
  • You stream from a phone or want multiple cameras, and a desktop-first tool does not fit.
  • You prefer to keep alerts and overlays with one vendor and the encoder with another.

The options, in order

  1. 1

    OBS Studio

    The free open source baseline

    Free option
    Open source

    OBS Studio is free and open source software for recording and live streaming on Windows 10 and 11, macOS 13.0 or newer, and Linux. Scenes are built from sources such as window captures, browser windows, webcams and capture cards.

    Many streamers move here for control and zero cost. You give up the ready-made themes, alerts and tips that Streamlabs bundles, and add those through plugins or other services.

    Checked on obsproject.com

    Visit OBS Studio

  2. 2

    Meld Studio

    Free multistreaming and 4K recording

    Free option
    Free

    Meld Studio is free streaming and recording software with multistreaming to every platform at once and 4K recording. Its Meld Spark tool covers interactive overlays and alerts, so the overlay layer is part of the same family.

    The pricing page lists a free plan and Meld Pro at $17 a month or $200 billed annually. A good fit if you want multistreaming without a paid tier.

    Checked on meldstudio.co

    Visit Meld Studio

  3. 3

    Prism Live Studio

    Multi-platform streaming plus a mobile app

    Free option
    Free

    PRISM Live Studio is free live streaming and recording software with multi-platform streaming and video editing. It runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and on macOS Monterey 12.3 or higher on Apple Silicon.

    A Plus subscription and Credits are sold in the mobile app. Consider it if you also stream from a phone and want one family of tools across both.

    Checked on prismlive.com

    Visit Prism Live Studio

  4. 4

    Lightstream

    Browser-based streaming without downloads

    Free option
    Free

    Lightstream is cloud-native streaming software that runs in the browser with no download. The free tier has a Lightstream logo, 720p, a 10 hour limit per stream and up to 3 scenes.

    Premium is $12, or $9 a month annually, and removes the logo while adding 1080p and 20 scenes. It simulcasts to up to five destinations.

    The cloud approach spares your PC but caps quality on the free tier.

    Checked on golightstream.com

    Visit Lightstream

  5. 5

    StreamElements

    Overlays and a bot from a rival suite

    Free option
    Unknown

    StreamElements is not an encoder in the same sense. It is a cloud platform with overlays, tipping, a chatbot, alerts and merchandise, plus SE.Live, a plugin for OBS Studio.

    Streamers who leave Streamlabs often pair OBS Studio with StreamElements, which keeps the overlay habit while changing the encoder. Our StreamElements alternatives page covers that platform in turn.

    Checked on streamelements.com

    Visit StreamElements

  6. 6

    Larix Broadcaster

    Phone-based contribution streaming

    Free option
    Free

    Larix Broadcaster is a free mobile broadcasting app from Softvelum for iOS and Android, aimed at remote contribution streaming. It is not a PC streaming suite and has no overlay layer.

    It suits a second camera or a field feed rather than a gaming setup. Treat it as an addition to your streaming stack more than a replacement for desktop software.

    Checked on softvelum.com

    Visit Larix Broadcaster

  7. 7

    Switcher Studio

    Multi-camera streaming to several platforms

    Free option
    Free trial

    Switcher Studio is multi-camera live streaming software that lets you stream to several platforms. Its homepage and pricing page offer a 14-day free trial.

    It leans toward camera switching and productions with more than one angle than toward single-screen game capture. Look at it if your streams include a camera crew or IRL segments, and check plan prices on the vendor site.

    Checked on switcherstudio.com

    Visit Switcher Studio

  8. 8

    NVIDIA App

    Capture and broadcast tools for GeForce owners

    Free option
    Unknown

    The NVIDIA App is the driver companion for PC gamers and creators. NVIDIA lists ShadowPlay and the Broadcast App among its features.

    It does not replace a full streaming program with scenes and alerts, but for recording gameplay and cleaning up a microphone or camera it covers ground that streamers otherwise add through software. It only applies to NVIDIA graphics cards.

    Checked on nvidia.com

    Visit NVIDIA App

  9. 9

    Medal

    Clipping your gameplay for sharing

    Free option
    Free

    Medal records, edits and shares gaming clips and gameplay videos, and its homepage describes it as free on PC. It does not stream, so it complements Streamlabs Desktop alternatives instead of replacing them.

    If you are leaving the Streamlabs ecosystem, Medal covers highlights and sharing while a separate encoder handles live output.

    Checked on medal.tv

    Visit Medal

Choosing among these

Decide whether you want an encoder, a platform or both. If cost and control matter most, begin with OBS Studio and add services as needed.

If multistreaming is the draw, compare Meld Studio and Lightstream, remembering that Lightstream's free tier is capped at 720p. If a second device is in play, look at the phone and multi-camera tools.

Frequently asked questions

What is Streamlabs Desktop?

It is streaming software from Streamlabs, which describes itself as a platform for Twitch, YouTube and Facebook with Streamlabs Desktop, alerts, overlays, tipping and merch. The company's pricing page states it takes zero cut of your donations.

Is there a free alternative with multistreaming?

Meld Studio lists a free plan at $0 a month with multistreaming and 4K recording, and PRISM Live Studio offers multi-platform streaming in a free desktop app. Lightstream also has a free tier, limited to 720p and a 10 hour stream length.

Can I stream without installing software on my gaming PC?

Lightstream runs in the browser and needs no downloads, according to its homepage. The free tier carries its logo and 720p, and Premium removes the logo and adds 1080p.

Which option is open source?

OBS Studio describes itself as free and open source software for recording and live streaming. Most of the other tools here are closed products with free or paid tiers.

Do I lose my alerts if I switch?

Alerts come from the platform, not the encoder. StreamElements pairs with OBS Studio through its SE.Live plugin, and Meld offers Meld Spark for overlays and alerts, so you can keep an alert layer.
