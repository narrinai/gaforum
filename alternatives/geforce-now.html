GeForce NOW Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

GeForce NOW is NVIDIA's cloud gaming service, which streams games from stores you already use, such as Steam, Epic, GOG, PC Game Pass and Ubisoft Connect, on GeForce RTX servers. It is sold as Performance and Ultimate memberships.

People look past it when they would rather pay for a library than bring their own, bundle cloud play with a subscription they already have, or rent a full Windows PC. This page covers eight such services, using only what each vendor states on pages we opened.

Prices are mostly missing because the pages did not show them in readable text. See the cloud gaming services ranking for the wider field.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Vendors can pay for more visibility; how that works is explained in our disclosure, linked above every ranking. No commission is earned on clicks or purchases, and we do not work as an affiliate.

Why teams look beyond GeForce NOW

  • GeForce NOW streams games you own in connected stores, so it does not replace a catalog; services like Blacknut and Xbox Cloud Gaming bring a library with the subscription.
  • Its Install-to-Play option is available only to Performance and Ultimate members, which matters if you want a lower tier.
  • The membership page did not show prices in readable text, so budgeting takes an extra step.
  • Players who want a whole PC, not just games, can rent one through Shadow.
  • Households that already pay for Prime or Game Pass may get cloud play without a new subscription.

The alternatives

  1. 1

    Xbox Cloud Gaming

    Cloud play inside Game Pass

    Free option
    Unknown

    Xbox Cloud Gaming is the cloud part of Xbox Game Pass and runs on Xbox consoles, PC and other devices. It suits players who want a catalog of games included rather than streaming titles they bought elsewhere.

    You give up GeForce NOW's connection to existing store libraries, and the play.xbox.com page showed almost no readable text, so check the device list yourself.

    Visit Xbox Cloud Gaming

  2. 2

    Shadow

    A full Windows PC in the cloud

    Free option
    Unknown

    Shadow rents a full Windows PC in the cloud that you reach from a browser or from your devices, with a Shadow Drive for storage. That is more than a game streamer: the PC can also run creative or work software.

    The cost of that flexibility is setup work, because you manage the machine yourself. It lists regional pages for Europe, North America, France, Germany, the United Kingdom and Spain.

    Checked on shadow.tech

    Visit Shadow

  3. 3

    Boosteroid

    Browser and app play on many systems

    Free option
    Unknown

    Boosteroid runs games in a browser or through apps on Windows, Android, macOS, iOS and Linux, so nothing needs downloading or updating on your side. It says any subscription includes unlimited gaming time.

    That makes it an alternative when your hardware is varied, though the plan prices were not available to us.

    Checked on boosteroid.com

    Visit Boosteroid

  4. 4

    Amazon Luna

    A rotating library with Prime

    Free option
    Unknown

    Amazon Luna offers a changing library of games and party games through GameNight, needing no downloads or console. The Dutch page we opened says it is included with Prime.

    It fits casual and family play more than a library you already own, and the rotating catalog means a title can leave.

    Checked on luna.amazon.nl

    Visit Amazon Luna

  5. 5

    PlayStation Plus cloud streaming

    Cloud streaming for PlayStation fans

    Free option
    Unknown

    PlayStation Plus lists cloud streaming among its benefits and offers hundreds of PS4 and PS5 games with online multiplayer across Essential, Extra and Premium plans. It suits players already in Sony's ecosystem.

    It does not stream your PC store library, and the device list for streaming was not captured.

    Checked on playstation.com

    Visit PlayStation Plus cloud streaming

  6. 6

    Blacknut

    A household library subscription

    Free option
    Unknown

    Blacknut says it has more than 1,000 games, no playtime limit and new titles every month, playable on PC, Mac, smartphones, tablets and Smart TV. It is aimed at households.

    Choose it if you want a catalog included in the fee, and know that you cannot bring your own purchases.

    Checked on blacknut.com

    Visit Blacknut

  7. 7

    Antstream

    Retro games

    Free option
    Unknown

    Antstream Arcade streams more than 1,300 officially licensed retro games, with titles from publishers such as Atari, Bandai Namco and Disney, on PC, console, mobile and TV. It is a niche alternative.

    It will not cover modern PC releases.

    Checked on antstream.com

    Visit Antstream

  8. 8

    Parsec

    Streaming your own PC

    Free option
    Free trial

    Parsec is a remote desktop app that lets you connect to a computer you own, rather than renting cloud hardware. It has a free trial and lists a 4:4:4 color mode in its professional plan.

    It fits players who already have a capable PC at home. Your own machine and upload speed set the quality.

    Checked on parsec.app

    Visit Parsec

How to pick between them

Decide whose library you want. If it is the games you already own on PC stores, stay with GeForce NOW or use Parsec against your own computer.

If you would rather rent access to a catalog, compare Xbox Cloud Gaming, Blacknut, Luna and PlayStation Plus by which games you play. If you need an entire Windows desktop, try Shadow.

Since many plan prices were not readable, check current figures on each site, and test streaming at your own times of day.

Frequently asked questions

Do these services let me play games I already bought?

GeForce NOW streams games from connected stores such as Steam and Epic. Among the alternatives, Parsec connects to your own PC and Shadow gives you a Windows PC where you can install them. Library services like Blacknut or Luna use their own catalogs.

Which alternative may cost nothing extra?

Amazon Luna is described on its Dutch page as included with Prime, and Xbox Cloud Gaming is part of Game Pass, which has its own fee. Check availability in your country first.

Can I play on a browser?

Boosteroid runs in a browser on Windows, Android, macOS, iOS and Linux, and Shadow offers a Launch in Browser option. Other services depend on their apps or devices.

Are there options for retro games?

Antstream Arcade streams over 1,300 licensed retro titles. It is a separate niche from modern PC cloud play.

Why are prices missing?

Several vendor pages did not show prices in text our fetcher could read. We left them out instead of guessing, so open the current pricing page before subscribing.
