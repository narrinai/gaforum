Sensor Tower Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Sensor Tower is a digital market insights platform covering mobile apps, advertising, web, retail media and gaming, with product areas such as Pathmatics, Web Insights and Gaming Insights (GameIQ). Its home page offers a free sign-up and a free Chrome extension but lists no paid prices, only Request Demo and Contact Sales.

A smaller studio may want narrower data for less. The eight alternatives below range from app store trackers to Steam and console databases, each described from its own pages.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Sensor Tower

  • Paid Sensor Tower prices are not listed, so a studio cannot size the cost before a sales conversation.
  • Sensor Tower covers advertising, web and retail media as well as apps, which a team that only needs game rankings may never use.
  • A PC or console studio gains little from app store panels and may prefer Steam or console datasets such as Gamalytic or GameDiscoverCo.
  • Teams focused on store listings and ads can buy ASO tooling instead of full market intelligence.
  • One option on this list, Video Game Insights, is hosted on Sensor Tower's own domain, so independent data needs another pick.

The alternatives

  1. 1

    AppMagic

    Mobile market research on a build-your-own plan

    Free option
    Unknown

    AppMagic is a mobile app market intelligence service built for market research, with genre classification, comparison of apps and publishers, and detection of successful soft launches. Its pricing page lets you choose your own feature set.

    You gain a modular purchase that can keep a small budget small; you give up Sensor Tower's advertising and web data, and you will need to open the pricing page to see actual amounts.

    Visit AppMagic

  2. 2

    Appfigures

    Indie developers tracking their own and rival apps

    Free option
    Unknown

    Appfigures tracks app downloads, revenue, rankings, reviews and keywords, with live App Store and Google Play data. It reports 28M apps and 11B downloads tracked this year, self-reported with no stated method.

    You gain a free way to start and a developer-friendly scope; you give up the broad advertising and retail media coverage Sensor Tower lists, and plan limits are not described on the homepage.

    Checked on appfigures.com

    Visit Appfigures

  3. 3

    Apptopia

    Investors, brands and sales teams buying data

    Free option
    Unknown

    Apptopia sells mobile app consumer activity data and says more than 200 leading firms trust it. Its investor dataset maps 3,500+ tickers across 160+ countries.

    The model is quote-only, so it is closer to Sensor Tower's enterprise end than to a budget tool. You gain finance-grade ticker mapping; you give up an easy self-serve entry, which matters if the point is spending less.

    Checked on apptopia.com

    Visit Apptopia

  4. 4

    AppTweak

    App store optimization and Apple Search Ads

    Free option
    Free trial

    AppTweak is an app store marketing and intelligence platform for ASO and Apple Search Ads, and its homepage offers an MCP connection to bring AppTweak data into ChatGPT and Claude. Pay-as-you-go language suits a team that wants to test before a long contract.

    You gain keyword and store-page focus; you give up market-size and advertiser-spend data.

    Checked on apptweak.com

    Visit AppTweak

  5. 5

    MobileAction

    Growth teams running Apple Ads

    Free option
    Unknown

    MobileAction is a growth platform for apps and games with MobileAction AI, a CMP for Apple Ads, ASO Intelligence and Ad Intelligence. The company says it was founded in 2013 in San Francisco and its footer calls it an InMobi Advertising company.

    You gain campaign tools alongside the data; you give up a visible price list and a broad market-sizing product.

    Checked on mobileaction.co

    Visit MobileAction

  6. 6

    Gamalytic

    Steam-focused revenue estimates

    Free option
    Unknown

    Gamalytic is a database of 100,000+ Steam games with estimated revenue, sales and player data, aimed at developers, market researchers and enthusiasts. The homepage states it is not affiliated with Valve or Steam.

    You gain Steam-specific estimates at game level; you give up mobile coverage entirely, and the revenue figures are estimates, not reported numbers.

    Checked on gamalytic.com

    Visit Gamalytic

  7. 7

    VGInsights

    Video game market research from one dataset

    Free option
    Unknown

    Video Game Insights is for developers, publishers and anyone doing video game market research, with 50+ datapoints per game across over 150,000 games. Its pages live on app.sensortower.com, so it looks like a Sensor Tower product rather than an outside rival.

    You gain game-level depth for PC titles; you give up the independence the word alternative suggests.

    Checked on app.sensortower.com

    Visit VGInsights

  8. 8

    GameDiscoverCo

    Console and Steam data with API access

    Free option
    Unknown

    GameDiscoverCo offers Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Switch data on sales, revenue, wishlists and audiences, plus MCP and API access that requires its Pro plan. Its homepage says over 110 teams use it, naming Bandai Namco, Krafton, Team17 and Raw Fury.

    You gain console coverage and an API; you give up a public price and mobile app data.

How to pick between them

Match the tool to the platform you sell on. Mobile studios can start with Appfigures for tracking or AppMagic for research, and add AppTweak when store optimization is the real job.

PC and console teams should look at Gamalytic and GameDiscoverCo. Apptopia suits those who buy data in bulk. Write down the three questions you actually ask each month, then request a trial or quote from two vendors and see which can answer them without extra modules.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cheaper alternative to Sensor Tower for mobile games?

Appfigures offers a Get Started Free button with no credit card, and AppMagic lets you build a plan from dashboards, tools and data access. Neither page lists plan prices in the captured text, so request current terms.

Which alternative covers Steam games?

Gamalytic covers 100,000+ Steam games with estimated revenue, sales and player data. GameDiscoverCo adds Steam plus PlayStation, Xbox and Switch data. Both treat figures as estimates, so use them to size a market, not to audit a rival.

Does Sensor Tower have a free option?

Its home page has a Sign Up for Free button and a free Chrome extension called Web Insights. Paid prices are not listed; the page offers Request Demo and Contact Sales for fuller access.

Is Video Game Insights independent of Sensor Tower?

Its pages are hosted on app.sensortower.com, so it appears to be a Sensor Tower product. It is useful for game-level data, but not a separate source if you want a second opinion.

Can I use these tools with an AI assistant?

AppTweak says it offers an MCP connection for ChatGPT and Claude, and GameDiscoverCo offers MCP and API access that needs its Pro plan. The other pages opened do not mention this, so ask each vendor.
