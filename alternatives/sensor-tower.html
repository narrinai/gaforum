Sensor Tower Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Sensor Tower is a digital market insights platform covering mobile apps, advertising, web, retail media and gaming, with product areas such as Pathmatics, Web Insights and Gaming Insights (GameIQ). Its home page offers a free sign-up and a free Chrome extension but lists no paid prices, only Request Demo and Contact Sales.

A smaller studio may want narrower data for less. The eight alternatives below range from app store trackers to Steam and console databases, each described from its own pages.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Sensor Tower

  • Paid Sensor Tower prices are not listed, so a studio cannot size the cost before a sales conversation.
  • Sensor Tower covers advertising, web and retail media as well as apps, which a team that only needs game rankings may never use.
  • A PC or console studio gains little from app store panels and may prefer Steam or console datasets such as Gamalytic or GameDiscoverCo.
  • Teams focused on store listings and ads can buy ASO tooling instead of full market intelligence.
  • One option on this list, Video Game Insights, is hosted on Sensor Tower's own domain, so independent data needs another pick.

    Appfigures

    Best for: Indie developers tracking their own and rival apps

    You gain a free way to start and a developer-friendly scope; you give up the broad advertising and retail media coverage Sensor Tower lists, and plan limits are not described on the homepage.

    Sources checked

How to pick between them

Match the tool to the platform you sell on. Mobile studios can start with Appfigures for tracking or AppMagic for research, and add AppTweak when store optimization is the real job.

PC and console teams should look at Gamalytic and GameDiscoverCo. Apptopia suits those who buy data in bulk. Write down the three questions you actually ask each month, then request a trial or quote from two vendors and see which can answer them without extra modules.

Frequently asked questions

What is a cheaper alternative to Sensor Tower for mobile games?

Appfigures offers a Get Started Free button with no credit card, and AppMagic lets you build a plan from dashboards, tools and data access. Neither page lists plan prices in the captured text, so request current terms.

Which alternative covers Steam games?

Gamalytic covers 100,000+ Steam games with estimated revenue, sales and player data. GameDiscoverCo adds Steam plus PlayStation, Xbox and Switch data. Both treat figures as estimates, so use them to size a market, not to audit a rival.

Does Sensor Tower have a free option?

Its home page has a Sign Up for Free button and a free Chrome extension called Web Insights. Paid prices are not listed; the page offers Request Demo and Contact Sales for fuller access.

Is Video Game Insights independent of Sensor Tower?

Its pages are hosted on app.sensortower.com, so it appears to be a Sensor Tower product. It is useful for game-level data, but not a separate source if you want a second opinion.

Can I use these tools with an AI assistant?

AppTweak says it offers an MCP connection for ChatGPT and Claude, and GameDiscoverCo offers MCP and API access that needs its Pro plan. The other pages opened do not mention this, so ask each vendor.
