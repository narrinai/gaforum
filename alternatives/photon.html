Photon Alternatives in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

Photon is a multiplayer development platform from Photon Engine with networking engines, voice and chat services and cloud hosting, and its homepage claims over 1.4 billion monthly players in the Photon Cloud. It does not publish prices on that page, only a Try Photon free sign-up and a mention of cost-effective plans.

Teams swap it for a self-hosted library, a different hosting model or a full backend. Here are nine alternatives and what each vendor says about cost, hosting and engine support.

How this list is ordered: by our editorial judgment of fit for the reader named above, based on vendor documentation, pricing and license pages opened on the review date. Why teams look beyond Photon

  • You want a networking library that carries no CCU cap or paywall, which FishNet states in its documentation.
  • Hosting should be yours: Colyseus deploys to AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, Fly.io, Railway or bare metal.
  • You want prices you can read before you integrate, as Colyseus Cloud (from $15 a month) does.
  • The project uses Unity and you prefer a first-party option, which Netcode for GameObjects is.
  • You need accounts, storage and matchmaking in the same product, as Nakama and Unity's live services offer.
  • A reactive database-backed approach suits your design better, which SpacetimeDB describes.

The alternatives

  1. 1

    Mirror

    Best for: Open-source Unity networking

    Pricing
    Not stated; open source with a Unity Asset Store download.
    Free option
    Open source

    Mirror is an open source networking library for Unity. Its homepage links to a Unity Asset Store download, to documentation on GitBook and to the source on GitHub, but names no license or price.

    Compared with Photon, you host the servers yourself, so you gain control and lose the managed cloud. It is a natural first look for Unity teams that already run their own infrastructure.

    • The homepage links to documentation at mirror-networking.gitbook.io/docs and to the source on GitHub. mirror-networking.com

How to pick between them

Split the choice in two. First, where will the servers run?

Managed cloud keeps you near Photon's model, so compare coherence and Normcore; self-hosting points to Mirror, FishNet, Colyseus or Nakama. Second, what do you need beyond netcode?

If it is accounts and storage, a backend such as Nakama or Unity's services belongs on the list. Ask each vendor for a quote based on your expected peak players, since several pages omit prices.

Frequently asked questions

Does Photon publish its pricing?

Its homepage does not. It shows a Try Photon free sign-up and mentions cost-effective pricing plans without figures, so you need the vendor's pricing pages or a quote. That is the main practical reason some teams look at libraries whose cost is stated plainly.

Which alternative has no player cap?

FishNet states no CCU caps and no CCU paywalls, and Normcore says no CCU caps and no hidden fees. Both are for Unity. A cap-free library still carries hosting costs, since you pay for the servers that run the game.

Is there a non-Unity option?

Colyseus targets Node.js and offers client SDKs, and Nakama and SpacetimeDB are backends rather than Unity plugins. Check each vendor's SDK list for your engine before you plan a migration, because this page only reports what the homepages state.

Can I keep voice and chat when leaving Photon?

Photon bundles voice and chat services with its networking engines. None of the alternatives here is described as a direct voice product, so plan a separate vendor if voice matters. Unity's live services list authentication and cloud tools but no voice product on the page we opened.

What is the cheapest published entry price here?

Colyseus Cloud starts at $15 a month per its homepage, and the Colyseus framework itself is free under MIT. Most other vendors on this list either are free libraries or do not publish plan prices on the page we opened.
