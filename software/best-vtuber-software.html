Best VTuber Software in 2026

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

VSeeFace comes first in our ranking of VTuber software, ahead of Warudo and VTube Studio. VTuber software tracks a streamer's face and body and drives a 2D or 3D avatar that appears on a livestream or video call.

What is VTuber software?

VTuber software turns webcam, phone or hotkey input into the movement of a digital avatar. The avatar is a 2D Live2D model, a 3D VRM model or a set of static images, and the program sends the result to OBS, Discord or a video call as a virtual camera or window capture.

How we ranked these

Picking avatar software looks like a taste decision, but the file format of the avatar you own settles most of it. A Live2D puppet, a VRM body and a pair of PNG images each need a different program, and several of the options below are free while others charge for commercial use.

This ranking read vendor homepages, manuals, itch.io listings and pricing pages and compared what each program says it supports: tracking method, model format, platforms, virtual camera output and integration with Twitch or YouTube. Nothing here comes from running the programs ourselves.

Where a price or a platform is missing from the pages we opened, the entry says so instead of guessing. The order favors a working streamer on a small budget who wants a believable avatar this month, with art tools placed last because they make models and do not run them live.

Model format you already own

Check this before anything else. VRM files suit VSeeFace, VMagicMirror, 3tene, Warudo and VNyan according to their pages, while Live2D models belong in VTube Studio, and PNGtuber images need an image swapper. A program that cannot load your file is a dead end, however good its tracking.

If you plan to commission a model, ask the artist which format they deliver and buy software to match. Converting between 2D and 3D is not something any vendor page we read describes.

How the avatar is tracked

Tracking can come from a webcam, an iPhone or a headset, or from no camera at all. VSeeFace documents iPhone tracking, 3tene V PRO adds VR headset and full-body control, and veadotube mini works from voice level and hotkeys. Webcam-only tools are the cheapest to start with, but a phone usually adds expression detail.

Pick the method that your desk, lighting and hardware can support every day, not the one that looks best in a demo clip.

Output into OBS and calls

The avatar has to reach the stream. Look for a virtual camera, a transparent window capture or a protocol such as VMC that passes motion to another program. VSeeFace lists a virtual camera and VMC, Animaze targets Twitch, Discord and Zoom, and Warudo mentions Twitch and YouTube integration.

If you also join video calls with the same avatar, a virtual camera is the feature that matters most.

Price and commercial use

Several tools here are free, one is name-your-own-price, and Animaze ties commercial rights to a paid tier for streamers earning $6,000 per year. Read the license page before monetizing a channel. Missing prices on a vendor page are a warning to check the store listing, such as Steam or BOOTH, before you plan around the cost.

Subscription costs for a modeling editor are a separate budget line from the live software.

    VSeeFace

    Free face and hand tracker for VRM avatars

    Why #1: VSeeFace costs nothing, tracks face and hands, takes VRM and VSFAvatar models and offers iPhone tracking plus a virtual camera.

    Best for
    VRM avatars on a zero budget
    Pricing
    Free.
    Free option
    Free
    Company
    VSeeFace
    Platforms
    Windows 8 and above, 64-bit only
    Key features
    Face tracking, Hand tracking, VRM and VSFAvatar support, Virtual camera, iPhone tracking, VMC protocol

    VSeeFace takes first place because the entry cost for a tracked 3D avatar is zero. The vendor describes it as a free, highly configurable program that puppeteers VRM and VSFAvatar models with face and hand tracking, and its manual documents a virtual camera, iPhone tracking and the VMC protocol.

    That combination lets a new streamer start with a webcam, move to an iPhone for finer expressions later, and pass motion data to other apps without buying anything. For a channel that has not yet earned money from streaming, that ladder of options is hard to beat.

    Where it falls short

    It runs on Windows 8 and above, 64-bit only, so Mac and Linux streamers are left out. The pages read describe no scene builder, props or chat-triggered events, so those need a second program.

    A streamer who wants chat-driven reactions and props should read the Warudo or VNyan entries instead.

What this list shows

Counting the ten programs here, three are described as free or name-your-own-price (VSeeFace, VNyan and veadotube mini), two more have a free edition next to a paid one (3tene and VMagicMirror), and Animaze and Live2D Cubism carry a free tier plus paid plans. Only two vendors, Animaze and Live2D Cubism, show any price figure or discount on the pages we opened. Most of the rest sell through itch.io, BOOTH or Steam, so the real price sits in a store listing, not on a company site.

Windows dominates. VSeeFace, VMagicMirror and VNyan name Windows only, VTube Studio is listed through Steam, and veadotube mini is the only program here with a stated Linux and macOS build. Kalidoface avoids the question by running in a browser.

Developers behind several of these tools are individuals or small teams, so documentation depth and update pace vary, and a streamer who relies on one for a living should keep a second option installed.

How to choose

1. Match the software to your model file

Open the folder of the avatar you own or plan to buy. A .vrm file points to VSeeFace, VMagicMirror, 3tene, Warudo or VNyan. A Live2D model points to VTube Studio.

A pair of PNG images points to veadotube mini. If you own nothing yet, start with a free VRM tool and a stock model so you can test lighting and camera angle before spending money on art.

2. Run a 20 minute setup trial

Install the free candidate, connect your webcam and send the avatar to OBS through its virtual camera or window capture. Watch for dropped frames, lip sync lag and what happens when you lean out of frame. Repeat with a phone as the tracker if the program supports one.

A short trial exposes the daily friction that a feature list hides, and it costs nothing with VSeeFace or the free tiers.

3. Read the commercial terms

Before you accept sponsorships or turn on subscriptions, read the license page of the program and of the model. Animaze names an income threshold for streamer rights, Live2D sells a PRO license through its store, and itch.io listings often leave the details to the author. Write down who owns the model, whether resale is allowed and what happens if the tool changes its terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best VTuber software in 2026?

VSeeFace is the best starting point in this ranking because it is free, tracks face and hands, loads VRM and VSFAvatar models and documents a virtual camera and iPhone tracking. Warudo is better for interactive shows with blueprints and plugins, and VTube Studio is better if your avatar is a Live2D model.

Can I become a VTuber without paying for software?

Yes. VSeeFace is free, VNyan and veadotube mini use name-your-own-price listings, and VMagicMirror and 3tene have free editions. You will still need a webcam and an avatar file.

Stock or free models exist, but a custom model is usually the largest cost, because it pays an artist for hours of rigging work.

Do I need an iPhone for VTuber tracking?

No. Webcam tracking is enough to start with, and every tracking program on this page is described as working with a camera. VSeeFace documents an iPhone option for more detailed expressions, so a phone is an upgrade for later, not a requirement on day one.

What is a PNGtuber and which tool fits it?

A PNGtuber uses flat images that swap when you speak, instead of a rigged model that follows your face. veadotube mini fits that style: its page describes switching between images as you talk, with blink images, hotkeys and Stream Deck and WebSocket control, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Which VTuber tools work on a Mac?

From the pages read, veadotube mini lists Windows, macOS and Linux, and Kalidoface runs in a browser, so it works wherever the browser does. VSeeFace, VMagicMirror and VNyan name Windows only. Check Animaze, VTube Studio and Warudo on their own pages for current platform lists, since those were not stated.

Can I earn money with an avatar made in this software?

Often yes, but read the terms first. Animaze describes Pro-Streamer commercial rights for streamers earning $6,000 per year. Live2D sells a PRO license through its store.

For itch.io and BOOTH tools, the author sets the terms. Model licenses from the artist are a separate document to check as well.

Do I need Live2D Cubism to stream as a VTuber?

No. Cubism Editor is for building 2D models, and many streamers buy a finished model from an artist instead. You need Cubism only if you rig models yourself or work as a rigger. The free edition continues after a 42 day PRO trial, which is enough to learn the basics.
