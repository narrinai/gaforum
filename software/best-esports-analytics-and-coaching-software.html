What is esports analytics and coaching software?

Esports analytics and coaching software helps players and teams improve by measuring performance and prescribing practice. The category spans stat trackers and overlays for League of Legends and Valorant, demo viewers and round analysis for Counter-Strike 2, aim trainers for first-person shooters, and marketplaces where coaches sell reviews and lessons.

How we ranked these

A player who wants to improve has three questions: what went wrong in the last match, what should I practice, and who can tell me. The eleven products here answer those questions in different ways.

Stat sites and overlays such as Blitz, U.GG and OP.GG answer the first for MOBA and tactical shooter players. Aim trainers like Aim Lab and KovaaK's own the second, and Refrag mixes review with practice for Counter-Strike 2.

Metafy answers the third with human coaches. We read vendor pages and pricing tables, noted what runs only in the browser versus a desktop app, and favored tools that show their cost and cover more than one use.

Pages that render with JavaScript are flagged where that limited what we could verify.

Which games each tool understands

Analytics tools are game-specific by nature. U.GG and OP.GG began with League of Legends, Leetify and Scope.gg center on Counter-Strike 2, STRATZ on Dota 2, and Blitz spans eight titles. Check that your main game is on the supported list before you weigh anything else, because a stat tool without your game is useless.

From numbers to a practice plan

Raw stats tell you what happened, but coaching software tells you what to do. Look for automated reviews after each match, round-level breakdowns, routine builders or lessons attached to weaknesses. Aim trainers add measurement of mechanical skill, and a coach marketplace adds a person who can see context a dashboard misses.

Team features and review tooling

Teams need shared access, scrim support, demo viewers and tactic detection, not only personal dashboards. Compare seat counts, replay tools and whether the vendor sells team licenses. One CS2 platform here costs more per month than most solo tools cost per year, and the audience is professional squads.

Pricing and the cost of free

Many products are free with ads and a premium tier. Read what the paid tier removes or adds: ad-free use, extra detail, demo access. Where a vendor shows no prices, treat the premium as unknown and test the free account first, since limits often appear only after sign-up.

What this list shows

Only two of the eleven tools print price figures that we could capture: Refrag (from $5.83 per month on annual billing) and Skybox Edge (from 350 euros per month). Five more offer a free entry route, namely Aim Lab, Blitz, U.GG, STRATZ and Scope.gg, and Aim Lab, Blitz, U.GG and STRATZ list paid tiers whose prices we did not find. Leetify, KovaaK's, OP.GG and Metafy gave no readable platform pricing, and Metafy lets creators set their own rates.

Counter-Strike 2 has the deepest tooling in this list, with four products aimed at it (Leetify, Scope.gg, Refrag and Skybox Edge), while League of Legends is served by Blitz, U.GG and OP.GG and Dota 2 by STRATZ. The trade-off is between automated feedback and human judgment: software scales cheaply but misses intent, and a coach on Metafy costs more per hour but reviews your decisions. Most improving players combine one stat tool, one trainer and occasional human review.

How to choose

1. Start with the game and the question

Name your main title and the one thing you want fixed: aim, positioning, utility use, draft or macro. A Counter-Strike 2 player with a utility problem gets more from a round-level reviewer than from an aim trainer, while a League of Legends player drafting champions needs a build site.

2. Run a free account for a week

Almost every consumer tool here has a free route. Link your account, play ten matches and check whether the output changes what you do next. If a report only repeats the scoreboard, move on before paying for a premium tier.

3. Pay for depth only when a team needs it

Solo players rarely need more than a stat site and a trainer. Teams with scrims and a coach can justify Refrag's team plan or Skybox's tiers because shared demos and tactic detection save hours of review. Compare the monthly cost per player rather than the headline.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best esports analytics and coaching software in 2026?

Aim Lab is first for most players: it is free to play, uses an AI coach, and offers 500 FPS game profiles, according to the vendor. Leetify is better for Counter-Strike 2 match reports, and Blitz is better for League of Legends, VALORANT and other titles with an overlay.

Which tool is best for Counter-Strike 2 improvement?

Leetify and Scope.gg give match reports and stats beyond the scoreboard, Refrag adds an AI coach with training routines, and Skybox Edge targets full teams with a 2D replayer and tactic detection. Pick by budget: Refrag starts at $5.83 per month, Skybox at 350 euros per month.

Are aim trainers worth paying for?

Start with the free versions. Aim Lab is free to play with an optional PRO membership, and KovaaK's offers player-created scenarios, though its price was not readable. Pay only if the paid tier adds something you used in the free week.

Can I hire a coach through these tools?

Metafy is a marketplace where creators and coaches share guides, coaching and events, with a hub for each game. Creators set their own prices. Software tools like Refrag and Leetify give automated feedback but do not sell human sessions in the pages we read.

Which League of Legends stat sites are free?

U.GG can be used as a guest, with a paid U.GG PLUS tier, and Blitz is free with an optional premium. OP.GG shows stats for League of Legends, TFT, VALORANT, PUBG and Overwatch with no price on the pages opened.

What do esports teams need beyond a stat tracker?

Teams need shared review: demo viewers, scrim tools, tactic spotting and multiple seats. Refrag's Team plan covers seven players, and Skybox Edge lists team statistics, voice comm integration and match reports in its higher tier.

Is there a Dota 2 option?

STRATZ offers Dota 2 statistics, match predictions and a GraphQL API, and the site is free to use according to the vendor. A STRATZ+ subscription exists but its price was not on the opened pages.
