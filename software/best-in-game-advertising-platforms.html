Best In-Game Advertising Platforms in 2026

What are in-game advertising platforms?

An in-game advertising platform inserts advertisements into the game itself, for example as billboards, posters or audio spots, and sells that inventory to brands. Unlike full-screen interstitials, native in-game formats aim not to interrupt play. Developers add an SDK, advertisers buy through a platform, and the vendor measures views and clicks.

How we ranked these

Studios that make PC and console games rarely want full-screen ads, which is why this niche exists. We looked at what each vendor says about placement style, supported platforms, engines, buying method and visible terms, using homepages, product pages, footers and published pricing.

We did not run campaigns or see revenue, and every performance figure on this page is the vendor's own. Entries that clearly place ads inside the game world sit at the top; publisher-side monetization and then pure mobile buying tools follow.

Bidstack and AdInMo were left out because their websites could not be opened during our review, and no readable page was available to verify them. Few vendors here show a price, so asking for terms is the main task for any studio on this list.

Native placement versus interruption

Look at where the ad appears. Gadsme says its ads are native in-world assets with no overlays, interstitials or pop-ups, and Frameplay describes intrinsic placements in game scenes. Venatus groups placements into in the game, around the game and away from the game.

Odeeo uses audio. Decide whether a poster in a street scene or a billboard in a stadium suits your art direction.

Platforms and engines

Check PC and console coverage in writing. Anzu lists Mobile and PC and Console solutions, and Gadsme names Unity, Unreal and C++ engines. Odeeo and Bigabid are mobile.

Kayzen targets mobile apps. Playwire lists websites, mobile and desktop apps.

Venatus lists websites, apps and browser games. An SDK for your engine matters more than a long feature list.

Buying model and demand

Programmatic means advertisers buy through an exchange, direct means campaigns are sold by hand. Anzu works programmatically on standard IAB sizes. Gadsme lists both.

Venatus mentions direct sales and header bidding, and Playwire offers managed and self-service routes with 15+ demand partners on mobile. More demand paths raise fill, but also add moving parts.

Fees you can read

Only Kayzen prints prices, with plans from $1,250 to $15,000 a month, and it is a buying tool. Every publisher-side platform asks for a demo, an application or a contact request. Before any integration, ask about revenue share, minimum traffic, payment schedule, brand safety controls and who owns the player data collected.

What this list shows

Of the ten platforms, only one (Kayzen) publishes plan prices on the pages we opened, and none of the publisher-side vendors shows a revenue share. Two list PC and console (Anzu and Gadsme), while Odeeo, Bigabid and Kayzen are mobile-only. Azerion describes itself as European and states 17 countries; Odeeo gives its location as Israel.

Gadsme says it was founded in early 2020, and Anzu dates its mission to 2017.

The category mixes two kinds of company. One kind builds ad units inside the game world and sells them to brands. The other is a general advertising or acquisition business that touches games.

Ranking prefers the first kind for PC and console studios, because the second kind rarely describes native formats. Vendor claims such as ARPDAU uplift, time in view and recall boosts come without independent data in the pages we read, so test one placement in a live build and measure retention before expanding.

How to choose

1. List your platforms and engine

Write down whether you ship on PC, console, mobile or browser, and the engine you use. Drop vendors that list none of them. Ask for an SDK for your engine and version, and for a build you can drop into a test scene before talking about money.

2. Pick the format you can live with

Decide whether in-world posters, clickable units or audio spots suit your game. Ask each vendor for example screenshots from games like yours, and for the list of ad categories and brands that can be blocked. A realistic racing game and a stylized platformer need different placements.

3. Ask for terms and run a small pilot

Request revenue share, payment dates, minimums and data policies in writing. Then run a limited pilot in one level or one region and compare retention and session length against a control. Only widen the rollout if player metrics hold steady.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best in-game advertising platform in 2026?

Anzu is the best starting point for most PC and console studios because it lists mobile, PC and console and buys programmatically on standard sizes. Gadsme is better when native in-world placements and named Unity and Unreal support matter, and Frameplay suits teams that want a self-service platform.

Do in-game ads hurt player retention?

Vendors say native placements avoid interrupting play, and Gadsme claims zero impact on retention, but these are vendor claims with no published method. Test with a limited release and compare retention with and without the placement before committing.

Which platforms support PC and console games?

Anzu lists PC and console solutions, and Gadsme states mobile, PC and console with Unity, Unreal and C++ engine support. Playwire lists desktop apps, and Venatus lists browser games. Odeeo, Bigabid and Kayzen are mobile focused.

Why are Bidstack and AdInMo missing?

Both vendors' sites could not be opened during our review (one returned a not-found error, the other a server error), so we had no readable page to verify. We rank only vendors whose pages we could read.

How much does in-game advertising cost for developers?

For publishers, it is usually free to integrate and the vendor takes a share, but none of the publisher-side pages we read states one. Kayzen, a buyer-side tool, lists $1,250, $5,750 and $15,000 monthly plans. Ask for revenue share in writing.

What is the difference between in-game and rewarded mobile ads?

In-game ads appear as part of the scene, such as a poster, and do not stop play. Rewarded mobile ads are full-screen videos that a player chooses to watch for a reward. Odeeo's audio ads are a third format, built for quiet moments.

Is Azerion a good fit for a European studio?

Azerion calls itself Europe's leading digital advertising company and offers Game Distribution alongside advertising. Its pages speak mostly to advertisers, and no pricing or native format is described, so ask what it offers for your platform.
