What are mobile attribution tools for games?

A mobile attribution tool, often called a mobile measurement partner, matches each install and later in-game event to the ad campaign that drove it. It receives postbacks from ad networks, reads Apple's SKAdNetwork signals on iOS, filters fraud, and reports cost, revenue and retention by channel so a studio can decide where the next marketing dollar goes.

How we ranked these

A game studio that buys installs needs to know which campaign produced paying players, not only downloads. That question gets harder on iOS, where Apple limits user-level data and attribution runs through SKAdNetwork.

This page ranks ten tools by how well they answer it for small and mid-size studios. Seven are measurement partners in the strict sense.

Three, Apple Search Ads, Moloco and Kayzen, are advertising platforms that report their own results, and they sit at the bottom for that reason. We read vendor homepages, pricing pages and about pages, and compared what each says about platforms covered, plans and fee structure.

We did not run campaigns or send test installs. One planned vendor, Adjust, could not be opened during our check, so it is not ranked; Affise was added in its place after we read its pages.

Price curve for a small install budget

Attribution fees often grow per conversion or per tracked event, which punishes a game that tracks many in-app events. Look at the starting plan, what is included for free, and what happens past the included volume. A free tier or a trial lets you validate your setup before paying.

Be careful with flat monthly minimums: if your spend is small, the tool can cost more than the ads it measures. Ask whether sandbox events count toward usage.

Coverage beyond phones

Many studios now ship on PC, console and the web as well as mobile, and want one measurement view. Some vendors name PC, console and connected TV attribution in their pages; others stick to apps and web. If you run cross-platform campaigns or cross-promote between a mobile title and a Steam release, check that the tool can tie those journeys together.

Also check how deep linking works, since it drives reattribution when players return from a link.

Handling of SKAdNetwork on iOS

Apple's privacy framework turns attribution into modeled, delayed and aggregated data. Vendors differ on how they map conversion values to in-game revenue and retention events, and how clearly they explain the delay. Look for named support for SKAN, any consent management features and a plain explanation of conversion value setup.

A tool that treats SKAN as a first-class feature spares you from writing the mapping logic and re-explaining the gaps to your marketing team.

Fit with your ad stack and data pipeline

Attribution only helps if the networks you buy from send data to it and your analysts can reach the output. Check the integration list, cost aggregation, raw data exports and API access. Some tools expose data to AI assistants through an MCP server, which can help small teams ask questions without building dashboards.

Ad platforms that measure themselves are fast to start but do not compare channels fairly, so decide early whether you need a neutral third party.

What this list shows

Five of the ten tools show a price or a free allowance on pages we opened: AppsFlyer (a free Zero plan with 12K free conversions in the first year, then $0.07 per conversion on Growth), Tenjin (from $200 per month), Airbridge (from $40+ per month after a trial), Kayzen (three plans from $1,250 per month) and Affise (Performance plans from $499 per month yearly, though that product is affiliate tracking). Kochava lists a free tier without figures. Singular, Branch, Moloco and Apple Search Ads give no attribution prices, and Apple charges per tap instead.

The trade-off is neutrality against convenience. A dedicated measurement partner compares channels without a stake in the spend, but you pay per conversion or per month. Ad platforms such as Apple Search Ads, Moloco and Kayzen report inside their own walls and are cheaper to begin, yet they are the seller grading its own work.

Only Airbridge, headquartered in Seoul, names a non-US base on its pages. Vendor-reported figures such as Branch's 3 billion-device network or Airbridge's 1,000+ brands are claims, not audited numbers.

How to choose

1. Start from your monthly install spend

Estimate how many attributed conversions you will generate in a month and multiply by each vendor's rate. AppsFlyer's $0.07 per conversion after the Welcome Package, Airbridge's $0.0001 per data point after the included 500K, and Tenjin's $200 starting plan behave very differently at ten thousand versus ten million events. Ask quote-only vendors for the same calculation in writing, and include any fees for fraud protection, raw data or support tiers.

2. Test one iOS campaign end to end

Before you commit, run a small iOS campaign on a free tier or trial and check what the dashboard shows after the SKAN delay. Verify that conversion values map to your first purchase, day-seven retention or ad revenue event. If a vendor cannot show you the mapping and explain gaps in reporting, expect weeks of manual reconciliation later.

Ask which ad networks you plan to buy from already have integrations.

3. Decide how much of the stack you want in one place

Some tools add cost aggregation, LTV prediction, fraud prevention, ad monetization reporting or marketing mix modeling next to attribution. That consolidates vendors but raises switching cost. Others stay narrow and rely on your warehouse.

If your team is two people, a broader suite may save hours each week; if you have an analyst, a narrower tool with clean exports may do. Pick before you integrate the SDK, since removing it later means a game update.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best mobile attribution tool for games in 2026?

AppsFlyer is the broadest pick: a free Zero plan with 12K free conversions in the first year, attribution across mobile, web, CTV, PC and console, and fraud protection. Tenjin is better when a flat starting price from $200 per month and ad monetization reporting matter, and Airbridge is better for a lower entry price of $40+ per month.

Do I need an attribution tool if I only buy ads through Apple Search Ads?

Not strictly. Apple Search Ads reports campaign results on its own and offers an Attribution API. A third-party tool becomes useful once you run several networks, because it compares them under one set of rules.

Until then, Apple's own reporting can cover a single channel at no extra cost.

Is there a free mobile attribution option for a small studio?

Yes, in a few cases. AppsFlyer's Zero plan is free with a Welcome Package of 12K free conversions for the first year. Kochava's home page offers a free tier called Free App Analytics, and Tenjin says it is free to start with pay-as-you-grow attribution.

Airbridge offers a 30-day free trial before its $40+ Core plan.

What does SKAdNetwork mean for game marketers?

SKAdNetwork is Apple's framework for measuring ad-driven installs without user-level identifiers. Results arrive aggregated and delayed, so your team cannot see each player's path. Singular, Kochava and Branch name SKAN support on their pages, and you should check how each maps conversion values to purchases and retention.

Can attribution tools measure PC and console campaigns too?

Some can. AppsFlyer describes attribution across mobile, web, CTV, PC and console, and Airbridge lists mobile apps, web, PC, console and CTV. Singular's solutions menu includes PC and console as well.

If you cross-promote a mobile title to a Steam release, these are the vendors to compare first.

Are Moloco and Kayzen attribution tools?

Not in the measurement partner sense. Moloco runs AI-driven advertising for mobile apps and CTV, and Kayzen is a mobile-first demand-side platform. Both buy and optimize ads, and they report campaign results, but they are better paired with an independent measurement partner such as AppsFlyer or Singular than used instead of one.

How much does Kayzen cost compared with an attribution tool?

Kayzen is priced as a DSP subscription: Starter at $1,250 per month with up to $10,000 ad spend, Pro at $5,750 and Enterprise at $15,000, each with an overage percentage. That is a media-buying cost, not a measurement fee, so compare it to a tool like Tenjin or Airbridge only after deciding you need a DSP.
