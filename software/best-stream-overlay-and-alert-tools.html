What are stream overlay and alert tools?

Stream overlay and alert tools supply the graphics that sit on top of a live video: webcam frames, scene backgrounds, follower and donation pop-ups, goal bars and chat boxes. They usually run as browser sources inside a broadcasting app, and some also sell ready-made design packs or interactive viewer features.

How we ranked these

A channel's look usually comes from three places: an alert service that reacts to follows and tips, a pack of designed scenes, and a few widgets for goals and chat. The ten tools below cover all three, from cloud platforms that do everything to marketplaces that only sell art.

We read each vendor's homepage, pricing page and legal notice, noted what is free, and compared the published prices and revenue terms. We did not run any alert through a test stream, so nothing here claims latency or reliability.

Where a page showed no price, we say the price is unknown. Ordering favors a working streamer who wants good-looking scenes without paying for three separate subscriptions.

Free alerts versus paid design packs

Alerts that fire on a follow or tip are often free, while polished scene packs, animated stingers and matching emotes are sold. Work out which half you need. A channel with its own art can use a free alert service; a new channel without art may be better served by a subscription that bundles hundreds of designs than by buying single packs.

Cut taken from money viewers send

Tipping and interactive card systems sit between your viewers and your income. Some vendors say they take nothing from donations, others share revenue at a stated percentage, and some publish no terms at all. Read the revenue lines carefully, because a small percentage on every tip adds up over a year of streaming, and a flat fee may be cheaper for a busy channel.

How the overlay reaches your scene

Most tools give you a browser-source link that you paste into a broadcasting program, while others plug in directly or run as a viewer-facing extension on Twitch. Check which programs the vendor lists, whether a plugin is required, and whether the same account covers chat widgets and goals. Fewer moving parts means fewer things breaking mid-stream, and a single vendor to ask when one does.

Fit for the channel's size and style

A big esports-style channel needs consistent branding across scenes, alerts and panels; a small variety channel may only want a cheap template and a goal bar. We looked at how each vendor sells its assets, whether it offers a maker tool for custom changes, and how much of the catalog a free account reaches, since a free tier that shows only a sliver tells you little.

What this list shows

Three of the ten (StreamElements, Streamlabs and OWN3D) combine overlays with alerts, chat tools or tipping, while Lumia Stream and SAMMI add automation, lights or button control on top. Streamloots, Muxy and the Elgato Marketplace sell or run viewer-facing extras and assets instead. Published prices exist for Lumia Stream ($5.99 per month), Placeit (from $7.47) and the revenue split at Streamloots; Muxy sells on quote, and StreamElements, Streamlabs and Canva show no amount in the text we read.

What separates the ten is mostly who earns from what. OWN3D and Placeit sell art by subscription, Streamloots keeps a share of revenue (creators keep 80%, or 90% for Partners), and Muxy builds custom Twitch extensions for studios and publishers. SAMMI is the only one whose vendor calls it completely free.

Most tools list Twitch and YouTube, but Muxy centers on Twitch, so channels on other platforms have fewer choices there.

How to choose

1. Sort needs into alerts, art and interaction

Write three short lists: alerts you want (follows, tips, subscriptions), art you want (scenes, frames, stingers) and interaction you want (cards, buttons, lights). Most channels need the first two and skip the third. Each list points at a different tool, and the cheapest route is usually a free alert service plus one art subscription, bought only after the free part works.

2. Compare what you pay over twelve months

Convert every plan to a yearly cost and add any revenue share on tips. A subscription at a low monthly price can still cost more than a one-off pack, while a free tool with a percentage on donations can cost more than a flat fee once your tips grow. Use your real tip volume, even if it is small.

3. Test the link in your broadcasting app

Before paying, paste a free alert or overlay link into your broadcasting program, trigger a test event and watch how it appears. Confirm the font, animation length and sound all behave. If the vendor requires a plugin or a desktop client, install it now rather than ten minutes before going live, and note what you changed so a restore is easy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best stream overlay and alert tool in 2026?

StreamElements is our first pick for most streamers because it combines overlays, alerts, a chatbot and tipping in one cloud account with an OBS plugin. OWN3D is better if you want a large library of designs, and Streamlabs Desktop is better if you already use its broadcasting app.

Are stream alerts free?

Often, yes. SAMMI says it is completely free, Lumia Stream lists a free plan with Premium from $5.99 per month, and OWN3D says its chat widget, chatbot, donation page and goal bars are free for OWN3D Pro users. Paid tiers mainly unlock design packs and extra features.

Do alert services take a cut of donations?

It depends on the vendor. Streamlabs states on its pricing page that it takes zero cut of donations. Streamloots says creators keep 80% of subscription revenue, or 90% for Partners, and Partners keep 90% of every tip.

For others we found no stated percentage, so read the terms.

Where can I buy ready-made overlay designs?

OWN3D sells overlays, alerts, panels, emotes and badges, and its Stream Pass lists 500+ overlays and 12,000+ designs in its Overlay Maker. Placeit has Twitch templates and lower third banners from $7.47, and the Elgato Marketplace offers live designs and OBS scene collections.

Can Canva be used for stream graphics?

Yes, for static graphics such as banners and thumbnails. Canva describes itself as a free-to-use online design tool for social posts, presentations, posters and videos, with Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise plans. It does not run alerts or live widgets, so pair it with an alert service.

Which tools work beyond Twitch?

StreamElements lists Twitch, YouTube and Facebook Gaming. Lumia Stream lists overlays for Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok and Facebook, and OWN3D lists designs for Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Facebook and Trovo. Muxy centers on Twitch extensions, with Discord, TikTok and YouTube shown under reach.

Do overlay tools need OBS?

Mostly they work with it. SE.Live is a plugin for OBS Studio, SAMMI connects through OBS WebSocket, and OWN3D lists OBS Studio, Streamlabs and StreamElements as compatible. Streamlabs Desktop is itself a broadcasting app, so it needs no separate program.
