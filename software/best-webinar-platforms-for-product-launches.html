What are webinar platforms for product launches?

A webinar platform for product launches hosts a live presentation to a registered audience, collects sign-ups, supports polls and questions, records the session and passes attendee data to sales systems. Launch use puts weight on registration pages, simulive playback, replay hosting and CRM sync.

How we ranked these

A product launch webinar has a clear job: turn announcement interest into registered attendees, keep them watching, and hand sales a list of who engaged. Most generic webinar tools do the middle step well and leave the ends to other software.

This ranking covers ten tools that touch some or all of that chain, from full webinar suites to video hosts and one audience-interaction add-on. We read each vendor's pages for product descriptions, plan names, pricing, integrations and company details, and did not run a launch on any of them.

Where a number comes from the vendor, such as customer counts, the text says so. Order follows how much of the registration-to-replay chain each tool documents, then price clarity, then how much extra software it assumes.

Registration through replay in one chain

List the steps a launch needs: landing page, confirmation emails, live room, on-demand recording, follow-up. Zoom lists simulive and a production studio, Goldcast lists Content Lab and a Recording Studio, Airmeet lists custom landing pages, and Vimeo and Brightcove focus on hosting the replay. A tool that covers more of the chain means fewer imports and exports on a busy launch week.

CRM and attendee data

The value of a launch is the list of engaged leads. Zoom names CRM integrations among its features, Goldcast lists integrations, and Airmeet lists attendee analytics. Pages for Vimeo, Brightcove and Vbrick do not describe lead handoff, so a team using them should plan how names, questions and poll answers reach the CRM before the event.

Capacity and format fit

Zoom's entry Webinars plan covers up to 500 attendees; other pages did not state caps. Vbrick adds an eCDN for managed networks, which matters when thousands of employees watch together. Compare the plan cap with the registration count you expect, and check whether a simulive replay counts against any limits.

Cost clarity before the deadline

Not a single tool here prints a full webinar price table in the pages we read. Zoom names three plans without amounts, Slido lists plan types, and the rest use quotes or demos. When the launch date is fixed, a long sales cycle is a risk, so ask early for a trial or a quote and note which tools offer a free plan or a trial first.

What this list shows

Ten tools, three families. Webinar and event suites (Zoom Events and Webinars, Goldcast, Airmeet, Cvent, Bizzabo) cover registration and live rooms; video platforms (Kaltura, Vimeo, Brightcove, Vbrick) host and stream with webinar features of varying depth; Slido adds interaction only. Free access exists for Vimeo and Slido, free trials are mentioned for Goldcast, Airmeet, Cvent and Kaltura, and Zoom, Brightcove, Vbrick and Bizzabo show no trial on the pages we read.

The main trade-off is who owns the registrant list. Suites keep attendee data in one place and push it to a CRM, while video hosts leave registration and lead capture to other software. ON24, a well-known webinar name, could not be opened by our fetcher and is not ranked here, and Microsoft Teams live events is left out because Microsoft Learn says that format retired on June 30, 2026.

How to choose

1. Decide how leads will move

Before comparing features, decide which system receives registrants and engagement data. If the answer is a CRM already in daily use, shortlist tools that name integrations, such as Zoom and Goldcast. If marketing exports a spreadsheet, a simpler host like Vimeo may do.

Write the handoff down; a launch without it produces an audience and no follow-up.

2. Match capacity to your registration forecast

Take the registrations of your last big campaign and add a margin, then compare with plan caps. Zoom's entry plan stops at 500 attendees, and larger programs move up a tier. Ask quote-only vendors for the cap in writing, and for the cost of exceeding it, so the launch day does not hit a wall.

3. Plan the replay as a product

Many viewers arrive after the live session. Decide where the replay lives, whether it needs a gate, and who clips highlights. Vimeo, Brightcove and Kaltura are strongest at hosting; Goldcast and Airmeet describe clip tools.

Publish the replay within a day while interest is high, and keep captions on for accessibility.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best webinar platform for product launches in 2026?

Zoom Events and Webinars is our first pick: it lists simulive, a production studio, polls, Q&A, AI captions and CRM integrations in three tiers. Goldcast is better for B2B teams that want to turn the recording into clips, and Kaltura for companies that want launch video inside a wider library.

What is simulive and why use it for a launch?

Simulive plays a prerecorded session as if it were live, often with live chat and Q&A. For launches it removes the risk of a stumbling live demo while keeping the sense of an event. Zoom lists simulive among its webinar features.

How many attendees can a launch webinar hold?

It varies by plan. Zoom's entry Webinars plan covers up to 500 attendees, and higher tiers exist. Other vendors in this list did not state caps on the pages we read, so request the number in writing before booking.

Do these platforms sync with a CRM?

Zoom lists CRM integrations and Goldcast lists integrations, while Airmeet describes attendee analytics. Vimeo, Brightcove and Vbrick pages do not describe lead handoff. Verify which CRM objects are written, such as contacts, poll answers and attendance, before choosing.

Is there a free option?

Vimeo has a free plan and Slido's core features are free, though neither is a full webinar suite. Goldcast, Airmeet, Cvent and Kaltura mention free trials. Free options are good for rehearsal, but check caps before relying on them for the event day.

Where should the launch replay live?

On a platform whose player you control. Vimeo, Brightcove and Kaltura focus on hosting and delivery, and Vimeo also lists On Demand sales. Embed the replay on the product page, add captions and track views separately from live attendance.

Why is ON24 not in the ranking?

Our fetcher received an HTTP 403 error from its site, so we could not read facts from pages we opened, and we rank only tools whose vendor pages we read. Buyers who need it can request it separately from the vendor.
