What are virtual event streaming platforms for hybrid events?

A virtual event streaming platform for hybrid events runs a program that people attend in a venue and online at once. It streams the sessions, handles registration and agendas, and usually adds chat, Q&A, networking, sponsor booths and reports so organizers can see who watched what.

How we ranked these

A hybrid event fails in a particular way: the room gets the production budget and the remote audience gets a camera on a tripod. Multi-day conferences and game showcases need both groups to register once, watch the same sessions and reach sponsors in the same place.

We compared ten platforms by reading each vendor's own pages: product descriptions, pricing pages, plan structure, supported formats and company details. We did not run an event on any of them, and claims such as customer counts or analyst rankings are marked as the vendor's.

Weight went to tools that document both halves of a hybrid program, show a price or a clear route to one, and say how remote viewers take part rather than just watch.

Which half of the event it covers

Cvent, Accelevents, vFairs and Bizzabo document venue-side tools such as check-in, badge printing and mobile apps. Vimeo and Zoom Events and Webinars concentrate on the stream. Virtual PRO and Airmeet sit in between with booths, lounges and networking.

Decide whether your weak point is the room or the broadcast, then choose a platform that is strongest where you are thinnest.

Audience limits and program length

Virtual PRO publishes audience caps per event, from 1,000 to 40,000 across four plans, while Zoom's Webinars plan covers up to 500 attendees. Most others give no cap in the pages we read. For a multi-day showcase, check concurrent viewers, the number of parallel channels, and whether the plan allows unlimited events or sells them one at a time.

Price transparency and contract shape

Only Accelevents ($7,500 per event or from $13,500 a year) and Virtual PRO ($15,000 to $125,000 a year) print dollar figures. The rest sell through demos. A per-event license suits a one-off showcase; an annual one suits a calendar.

Ask whether seats, attendees or events drive the price, because each rewards a different program.

What happens to sponsors and data

Sponsors fund most hybrid programs. Bizzabo lists sponsor ROI, vFairs a lead capture app, Swapcard exhibitor analytics and Airmeet attendee analytics. Check that both onsite and online visits land in one report and sync to your CRM; Virtual PRO names HubSpot, Salesforce and Marketo for that.

What this list shows

Of ten platforms, only two publish dollar prices on the pages we read: Accelevents and Virtual PRO. Zoom lists three plan names without amounts, and the other seven offer a demo, a quote or a link to a pricing page we did not open. Free trials or free plans are mentioned for Goldcast, Airmeet, Cvent, Accelevents and Vimeo.

Three vendors state United States addresses (Airmeet, Cvent, Virtual PRO), and none of the ten states a European base in the pages opened.

The trade-off is breadth against depth. Suites such as Cvent and Bizzabo cover registration, venue and sponsors but describe streaming as one feature; video-first products such as Vimeo and Zoom stream well but leave registration and exhibitors to others. One naming wrinkle: the web address of Hubilo, a long-standing name in this market, now opens Virtual PRO from Brandlive, so older write-ups may describe a product that has since moved.

How to choose

1. Map the program before the tool

Write the agenda: how many days, parallel tracks, speakers in the room versus remote, sponsor slots. A one-day reveal with a single stage needs a stream and registration; a three-day show with booths needs exhibitors, lounges and meetings. This map decides whether to shortlist suites like Cvent and Accelevents or video-first options like Vimeo and Zoom.

2. Ask for the price in your event shape

Request a quote for your attendee count, number of events a year and admin seats. Compare Accelevents' per-event model with Virtual PRO's annual tiers, and ask the quote-only vendors to separate platform, production services and support. Insist on knowing what an extra event or an audience over the cap costs.

3. Test the remote experience as an attendee

Have a colleague who is not an organizer register, join from a phone and try Q&A, a booth visit and replay. Remote viewers are lost in the first minutes if login, audio or captions fail. Virtual PRO and Zoom list AI captions; confirm languages and accessibility for your audience before signing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best virtual event streaming platform for hybrid events in 2026?

Cvent is our first pick for large hybrid programs because it covers venue, registration, virtual experience and webinars in one vendor. Accelevents is better when you need a published price, and Virtual PRO when you run many multi-day events a year and want audience caps and booths spelled out.

How much does a hybrid event platform cost?

Few publish prices. Accelevents lists $7,500 per event or from $13,500 a year for unlimited events, and Virtual PRO lists $15,000, $50,000 and $125,000 a year across three tiers. Cvent, Goldcast, Airmeet, vFairs and others require a demo or quote.

Can one platform handle both the venue and the stream?

Cvent, Accelevents, vFairs and Bizzabo describe onsite tools such as check-in, badging and mobile apps alongside virtual features. Vimeo and Zoom describe the stream only, so a venue workflow would need a second system.

Do these platforms support sponsor booths?

Several do. Virtual PRO lists sponsors, exhibitors, booths and lounges, Airmeet lists exhibition booths, Swapcard exhibitor management and ROI analytics, and vFairs a floor plan builder with a lead capture app. Check how leads reach your CRM.

Is Zoom enough for a hybrid conference?

For a single-stage event with a few hundred viewers, often yes. Zoom's Webinars plan covers up to 500 attendees with polls, Q&A and AI captions. For multi-day programs with booths, sponsors and onsite check-in, a dedicated event platform is a better fit.

What happened to Hubilo?

Its former web address now opens Virtual PRO, an enterprise virtual and hybrid event platform from Brandlive, according to the company page. We list Virtual PRO on the facts we could read and do not assume any earlier Hubilo terms still apply.

How should I prepare a game showcase as a hybrid event?

Keep one registration for both audiences, rehearse the stream with the real speakers, and decide how remote viewers ask questions. Plan a replay page and clips afterward, since Goldcast and Airmeet both describe tools for repurposing recordings.
