What is voice changer and streaming audio software?

Streaming audio software controls what viewers hear. It mixes a microphone with game, music and chat sources, removes background noise, routes each source to the right output and sometimes changes the voice itself. Some tools run live between your devices and OBS, while editors such as Audacity work on recordings afterward.

How we ranked these

Bad audio loses viewers faster than bad video, yet the tools for fixing it come in four separate groups: mixers, noise removers, voice changers and editors. A streamer who buys the wrong group ends up with a funny voice and a noisy fan, or a clean signal and no way to route Discord separately.

This ranking read vendor pages, terms and pricing pages for eleven products and judged them on what each is for, what it costs, which operating systems it supports and how it reaches OBS or a call. We did not run them ourselves.

Free tools sit high because most streamers start on a budget, while services built for other jobs, such as meetings or speech synthesis, sit lower and say why.

Routing versus effects

Decide first whether the problem is where sound goes or how it sounds. Elgato Wave Link and Voicemeeter route sources into separate channels. NVIDIA Broadcast and Krisp clean a voice.

Voicemod and MorphVOX change it. Few products do all three, so a typical setup pairs a mixer with one effect tool.

Buying an effects app to solve a routing problem is the most common error.

Operating system and hardware

Platform lists differ sharply. Voicemeeter and SteelSeries Sonar are Windows programs, Wave Link and Voicemod list Windows and Mac, and Audacity and REAPER add Linux. NVIDIA Broadcast depends on Windows drivers for NVIDIA hardware.

Confirm your system on the vendor page, because a Mac streamer who downloads a Windows-only mixer loses an afternoon.

Virtual devices and latency

Most live tools install a virtual microphone or audio device so OBS and Discord can see them. That is convenient, and also a place where audio breaks after an update. Look at how many virtual devices a tool adds and whether it offers ASIO or WASAPI drivers, as Voicemeeter lists.

Fewer hops usually means less lag between your voice and the face on screen.

License terms and extras

Wave Link is free but sells plug-ins separately, Voicemeeter asks for a donation after 30 days, Voicemod gates some content behind PRO, and Krisp bills per user. Read what stays free and what turns into a prompt. Check the terms for commercial use if the channel earns money, as free hobby licenses do not always carry over.

    Free mixer with soundboard and dual-PC audio

    Best for
    Streamers on Windows or Mac
    Pricing
    Free for Windows and Mac, per the Wave Link 3.5 text on the page. Some effects plug-ins are sold separately on the Elgato Marketplace (Windows only).
    Free option

    Platforms

    Wave Link opens the list because it covers the most daily needs for no charge. According to the vendor page, version 3.5 is free for Windows and Mac and adds a built-in soundboard, Smart Switch, Audio Flow and Wave Cast for dual-PC audio.

What this list shows

Of the eleven products on this page, five are free to use in some form (Wave Link, Voicemeeter, SteelSeries Sonar, Audacity and Voicemod's free download), one is a donationware model, and only three list real price figures on the pages we opened: Krisp, ElevenLabs and Respeecher. REAPER, NVIDIA Broadcast and MorphVOX show no license price. That gap makes budgeting harder than the large number of free options suggests.

The category also blurs. Krisp and ElevenLabs come from meetings and speech synthesis, REAPER and Audacity from music production, and SteelSeries Sonar from a hardware maker. Only Voicemod and MorphVOX are voice changers by trade.

Vendors based in Spain, the United States and elsewhere sell into the same market, and a stream setup usually combines two or three of these tools, so check that their virtual devices do not conflict.

How to choose

1. Name the audio problem in one sentence

Write down what is wrong: game sound drowns your voice, a fan hums, Discord leaks into the stream, or you want a different voice. Routing problems point to Wave Link or Voicemeeter, noise problems to NVIDIA Broadcast or Krisp, and character voices to Voicemod or MorphVOX. A one-line problem statement stops you buying a bundle of features you will never touch.

2. Test the free layer first

Wave Link, Voicemeeter, Sonar LP and Audacity cost nothing to try, and Voicemod has a free download. Install one candidate, send it to OBS and record a five minute test with game sound playing. Listen for hiss, delay and clipping on a different pair of headphones.

If it holds up, you have saved the budget for a microphone upgrade.

3. Keep a fallback input

Whatever you pick, set a plain microphone input in OBS as a backup. Virtual devices can disappear after a driver or app update, and a stream with no audio is worse than one with a slightly noisy mic. Save your mixer settings, note the version number and update tools between streams, not before one.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best voice changer and streaming audio software in 2026?

Elgato Wave Link is the best overall pick here: it is free on Windows and Mac, includes a soundboard, routing and dual-PC audio. Voicemod is better if you want live voice effects, and Voicemeeter is better on Windows when you need exact control over every route and driver.

Do I need both a mixer and a voice changer?

Only if you want both jobs done. A mixer such as Wave Link or Voicemeeter controls levels and routes sources. A voice changer such as Voicemod alters the sound of the voice.

Many streamers use a mixer alone, and add Voicemod for a themed segment or a gag.

Is there a free alternative to Voicemod?

For effects, the choices on this list are limited, since MorphVOX lists no price and Voicemod itself has a free download. For mixing, Voicemeeter and Elgato Wave Link are free. Audacity handles recorded voice edits for free as well.

Free voice effects live in a single app only with Voicemod's free tier.

How do I remove keyboard and fan noise from a stream?

Use an AI noise filter. NVIDIA Broadcast adds noise removal on Windows with a supported graphics card, and Krisp offers noise cancellation with a 7 day free trial. Place the filter before your mixer so only the cleaned signal reaches OBS.

A better microphone position helps more than any software.

Can Voicemeeter work on a Mac?

No. The vendor page lists Windows audio interfaces such as MME, WASAPI and ASIO, and no macOS version appears. Mac streamers can use Elgato Wave Link, which supports macOS with AU effects, or Voicemod, which the vendor lists for PC and Mac.

Is ElevenLabs a live voice changer?

Not according to the pages we read. ElevenLabs lists text to speech, speech to text, voice cloning, dubbing and sound effects, with APIs and SDKs. It suits narration and dubbing of finished content.

For changing a voice in real time during a broadcast, look at Voicemod or MorphVOX.

Do I need a DAW like REAPER to stream?

No. A digital audio workstation is for recording and producing music, podcasts and voice-over. REAPER offers a 60 day full evaluation, which is useful for trying production work, but live streaming needs only a mixer and perhaps a noise filter. Add a DAW if you edit audio away from the stream.
