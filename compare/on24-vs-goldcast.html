ON24 vs Goldcast: Which Fits Your Team in 2026?

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology
vs

ON24 and Goldcast both sell webinar software to marketing teams, and both lean on AI language in their headlines. ON24 calls its product an AI webinar and engagement platform that turns webinar data into sales-ready leads.

Goldcast calls itself an AI-first video content platform for B2B videos, webinars and events, with tools for repurposing content. Neither publishes prices on the pages we read, so the comparison here is about positioning, trial access and the details each vendor chooses to put on its site, which a launch team can use to decide whom to call first.

The short answer

Pick ON24 when the webinar is a lead engine and your sales team wants engagement data to follow up on. Pick Goldcast when you plan a content program around launches, with recordings, repurposing and a free trial to test the workflow.

Both sell by quote as far as we could read, so ask for a priced proposal that names the number of events, registrants and seats.

Pick ON24 if

Pick ON24 if the main outcome you measure is pipeline: engagement tracking and lead data that sales can act on after each launch webinar.

Pick Goldcast if

Pick Goldcast if you want a trial to start with, and plan to turn each launch event into clips, podcasts and follow-up content using one platform.

Lead engine or content program

ON24's homepage tagline says to turn webinar data into sales-ready leads and deeper engagement, and its key themes are webinars, engagement tracking and lead data. That wording points at the follow-up: who attended, what they watched, which accounts should hear from sales next.

For a product launch where the goal is a pipeline report on Monday, that is the relevant promise.

Goldcast's pages describe a broader video content setup. Its product list includes Goldcast Events, Content Lab and a Recording Studio, and it speaks of content repurposing and podcasts next to webinars.

A launch there is the first of many outputs, since the recording becomes clips, posts and follow-up material. Marketing teams that already plan a content calendar around launches will recognize the pattern.

How to get a price

Neither site gave us a number. ON24's opened pages do not publish pricing, and Goldcast's pricing page offers a free trial and asks visitors to get a demo for custom bundles.

The practical effect is the same: you will be on a sales call. Go in with the scope written down: events per quarter, expected registrants, how many presenters need seats and whether you need SSO.

Goldcast's enterprise offer lists team workspaces, SSO, custom reports, custom permissions and multi-language support, which gives you a checklist to ask ON24 about as well. Ask each vendor to price the same scenario so the quotes can sit side by side.

Trial access and proof

A free trial lets you test a platform without a sales commitment. Goldcast offers one on its pricing page.

We did not see a trial on ON24's pages, so a pilot would need to be negotiated. For a team that wants to run a small launch event before deciding, the trial is a concrete difference.

Each vendor also publishes some of its own material. Goldcast cites a 2026 B2B webinar benchmark report on its site, and ON24's about page footer lists offices in San Francisco, Charlotte and London.

Treat vendor-written research as marketing, and ask for customer references from companies similar to yours.

Picking for a launch calendar

Map your next three launches. If each one is a single large webinar with a hard lead target, shortlist ON24 first.

If you expect a series of events, an on-demand library and a steady stream of short clips, shortlist Goldcast first. Ask both for a sample report from a past launch so you can see what the sales team receives.

Finally, check integration with your CRM and marketing automation before anything else. The facts we hold do not cover integrations in detail, and a webinar platform that does not feed your CRM cleanly can undo the value of its engagement data.

Frequently asked questions

Does ON24 publish its pricing?

The ON24 pages we opened did not show prices. The homepage title calls it an AI webinar and engagement platform, and the tagline focuses on sales-ready leads. Contact the vendor for a quote that matches your event volume.

Does Goldcast offer a free trial?

Yes. Goldcast's pricing page offers a free trial and asks visitors to get a demo for custom bundles, so list prices are not published. Enterprise plans include SSO, custom reports and custom permissions.

What products does Goldcast include?

Its pages list Goldcast Events, Content Lab, Recording Studio, Goldcast Agents, Smart Events and integrations. It describes itself as an AI-first video content platform for B2B videos, webinars and events.

Where is ON24 based?

The footer on ON24's about page lists offices in San Francisco, Charlotte and London. We did not find a stated headquarters on the pages we opened.

Which platform suits a one-off product launch?

Both can host a launch webinar. ON24 emphasizes engagement data and leads; Goldcast emphasizes content repurposing and offers a free trial. Pick on what happens after the event, and request a priced proposal from each.
