StreamElements vs Streamlabs Desktop: Which Fits Your Team in 2026?

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology
vs

StreamElements and Streamlabs both give streamers overlays, alerts and tipping, and they differ mainly in where the software lives. StreamElements describes a cloud-based platform that plugs into OBS Studio through SE.Live, while Streamlabs ships its own streaming app, Streamlabs Desktop, next to its alerts, overlays, tipping and merch tools.

This page uses only what the two vendors state on their opened pages, and says plainly where those pages are silent.

The short answer

Choose StreamElements if you already use OBS Studio and want the overlay, chatbot and tipping layer to sit on top of it. Choose Streamlabs if you prefer one vendor for the streaming app and the alert tools, and zero cut of donations is a deciding point.

Plan prices were not readable on the opened pages of either.

Pick StreamElements if

you stay on OBS Studio, want a chatbot, merchandise tools and a multistreaming companion app in the same account, and like a cloud-managed setup.

Where the software sits

StreamElements calls itself cloud-based and integrated with the stream. Its SE.Live plugin works inside OBS Studio, so the capture and encoding stay in OBS while overlays, alerts and the chatbot are managed online.

Streamlabs offers its own app, Streamlabs Desktop, which is used with its alerts, overlays, tipping and merch. The practical result is one vendor and one login for the whole stack, at the price of moving away from a stock OBS Studio setup.

Alerts, overlays and tips

Both list overlays, alerts and tipping for the major streaming services. The difference worth reading is in the tipping terms.

Streamlabs' pricing page states that the company takes zero cut of your donations. StreamElements' opened page lists SE.Tips but does not state its terms.

Payment processors can still charge their own fees, so ask where each tip is processed and what arrives in your account. Do not assume that no cut from the vendor means no cost from the whole chain.

Extras and add-ons

StreamElements adds a chatbot, merchandise tools and Ground Control, a multistreaming management companion app. For a streamer who wants to run chat commands, sell merch and push to several services from one account, that list is useful.

Streamlabs lists merch next to alerts and tipping. The opened page does not name a chatbot or a multistreaming app, though the vendor may offer them elsewhere.

Check the live site for the current catalog.

Pricing, with caveats

The StreamElements home page does not show a price. The Streamlabs Desktop product page could not be read in full because it renders with JavaScript, so its plans are not recorded here.

Treat both as unknown until you open the live pricing pages. Compare what is free, what is behind a subscription and whether branding appears on free overlays.

Picking for your setup

Streamers who have invested in OBS Studio scenes and plugins gain the least friction from StreamElements. Newer streamers who want an app that arrives preconfigured may find Streamlabs Desktop simpler to start with.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need OBS Studio for StreamElements?

StreamElements offers SE.Live as an OBS Studio plugin, and its alerts and overlays can be managed online. The opened page does not state every supported encoder, so check the setup guide for your software.

Does Streamlabs take a cut of tips?

Its pricing page states that the company takes zero cut of your donations. Payment processors may charge their own fees, which the opened page did not cover, so read the payout terms before relying on a net figure.

Which one has a chatbot?

StreamElements lists a chatbot among its features. The Streamlabs page we opened does not name one, so confirm with the vendor if chat commands matter to you.

Can I use both?

Many streamers combine tools, but running two alert systems at once can duplicate notifications. Pick one for alerts and use the other only where a specific feature is missing.

What does each cost?

The opened pages did not give readable prices. StreamElements' home page shows none, and the Streamlabs Desktop page rendered via JavaScript. Visit each live pricing page and compare plans on the same date.
