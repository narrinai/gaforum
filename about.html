About GA Forum

GA Forum is an independent research site and discussion forum about the software used to make games, run livestreams and organize esports.

We publish rankings of 10 to 14 products per niche, head-to-head comparisons, alternatives pages, practical guides and data pages built from published sources. Each ranking names the reader it is written for, puts the products in order, and says what every product does badly. Next to the research sits a forum with boards for game development tools, livestreaming and esports, where readers can push back on our order.

Who it is for

Indie and mid-size studio leads, technical directors and producers; streamers, editors and the people who build their setups; tournament organizers, team managers and broadcast crews; and company event teams who need live video that holds up.

Who runs it

GA Forum is published by G-Cas B.V. in Utrecht, the Netherlands. The editorial team researches and writes the pages, with AI assistance; Sebastiaan Smits is the editor responsible for what appears.

The domain

ga-forum.com has a long past. From 2004 it hosted the Gaming-Age Forums, the board that grew into NeoGAF before moving to neogaf.com. GA Forum continues on the domain as a software research site and is not connected to those communities or their operators. The details, with archive links, are on the history page.

Money

Vendors can pay for more visibility; how that works is explained in our disclosure, linked above every ranking. No commission is earned on clicks or purchases, and we do not work as an affiliate. Details are on the disclosure page.

Contact

Corrections, tips and questions: contact form or hello@ga-forum.com. Vendors who want to be considered: get listed.
