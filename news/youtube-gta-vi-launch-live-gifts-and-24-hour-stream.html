YouTube adds GTA VI gifts to YouTube Live and plans a 24-hour launch livestream

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

On November 12, 2026, YouTube and Rockstar Games announced launch-week features for Grand Theft Auto VI, which launches November 19. YouTube Live is adding five limited-edition, game-themed gifts, with animations that appear on screen during streams, and eligible viewers of select creator streams can claim and send one for free while supplies last.

YouTube is also running a 24-hour launch livestream on youtube.com/youtube from November 19 at 12 AM ET, with a pre-show from 11 PM ET on November 18. The post lists live gameplay, creator podcasts and on-the-ground coverage from Miami.

A GTA VI Shorts effect and soundtrack playlists on YouTube Music round out the package.

YouTube cites its own data that the Grand Theft Auto series has over 900 billion views on YouTube, and that pre-launch GTA VI videos had passed 40 billion views.

Why it matters

For streamers, a major launch concentrates audience attention in a short window, and platform-run events can compete with individual channels for it. The gifts only apply to select creator streams, so most channels will not see the effect directly.

Teams that stream to several platforms should plan for demand spikes on launch day, including chat volume and stream stability. Our multistreaming platforms category and live streaming APIs category cover tools that help with distribution and capacity.
