PlayVS acquires LeagueSpot to expand league and tournament software

By GA Forum editorial team, edited by Sebastiaan SmitsLast reviewed November 18, 2026Methodology

PlayVS, which describes itself as the leading scholastic and collegiate competitive gaming platform in North America, announced on April 9, 2026 that it acquired LeagueSpot, a global esports league and tournament management platform. Terms were not stated in the release.

PlayVS says the deal adds league operations technology and international experience, and introduces more flexible, partner-led competition models, including white-label leagues and automated tournament operations at scale. The release says PlayVS supports thousands of schools and more than 200,000 students annually, and that LeagueSpot had worked with the PlayVS College League for several years.

Other LeagueSpot customers named include Epic Charter Schools, Gallaudet University, Boys & Girls Clubs and the YMCA.

LeagueSpot CEO Andrew Barnett will join PlayVS to lead global strategy, and LeagueSpot's technology and team will be integrated into the PlayVS platform.

Why it matters

For esports organizers, this is another case of a league operator absorbing a tournament software vendor. Schools, colleges and community organizers who run events on LeagueSpot should expect the product to be folded into PlayVS, and should ask about roadmap, data export and pricing before the next season.

The white-label and automation emphasis also signals where this software segment is heading: publishers and federations running many events with little manual admin. Organizers comparing options can start with our esports tournament management software category.
